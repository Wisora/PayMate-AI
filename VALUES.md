# Values – PayMate AI

Purpose
Articulate the cultural and ethical principles that guide PayMate AI’s contributors, governance, and investor relations.  
Provides a foundation for decision‑making, collaboration, and long‑term trust.

Core Values

1. Transparency

- Open governance binder with registers for risk, audit, compliance, and security.
- Clear communication of roadmap, metrics, and investor updates.
- Traceability of changes and decisions across the repo.

1. Integrity

- Adherence to compliance standards (GDPR, WCAG 2.1, multilingual).
- Responsible handling of audits, disclosures, and investor Q&A.
- Commitment to ethical scaling and financial accuracy.

1. Accessibility

- Inclusive design across documentation, decks, and product features.
- Multilingual support to ensure global reach.
- Continuous accessibility audits and improvements.

1. Innovation

- Rapid product ideation with AI‑driven workflows.
- Cost‑efficient scaling strategies from free tiers to enterprise adoption.
- Continuous improvement cycle through lessons learned and evolution tracking.

1. Inclusivity

- Contributor community built on respect and professionalism.
- Recognition of diverse roles in CONTRIBUTORS.md.
- Localized documentation and decks for multilingual inclusivity.

1. Accountability

- Governance cadence with quarterly and annual reviews.
- Registers for risks, audits, compliance, security, and changes.
- Clear ownership of actions and decisions logged in DECISIONS.md.

Review Cadence

- **Quarterly** → Values reaffirmed in governance binder.
- **Annual** → Values presented in board deck alongside vision and strategy.
