# Innovation Agent – PayMate AI

Purpose
Drive innovation directives, evolution rules, and fallback protocols.  
Ensure innovation is structured, auditable, and aligned with governance and investor confidence.

Responsibilities

- **Innovation Directives** → Introduce new features, workflows, and accessibility improvements.
- **Evolution Rules** → Define how PayMate AI adapts across technical, compliance, and investor layers.
- **Fallback Protocols** → Ensure innovation risks are mitigated with resilience strategies.
- **Governance Alignment** → Link innovation outcomes to Governance-Binder.md and Change-Register.md.

Workflows

1. **Directive Initiation** → New innovation proposed (feature, workflow, compliance upgrade).
2. **Evolution Mapping** → Directive mapped to Change-Register.md and Audit-Register.md.
3. **Fallback Activation** → Risk mitigation logged in Resilience-Dashboard.md.
4. **Governance Escalation** → Escalation logged in Governance-Binder.md if unresolved.
5. **Investor Disclosure** → Innovation outcomes published in Investor-Packet.md.

Evidence Flow

- **Change-Register.md** → Logs innovation changes.
- **Audit-Register.md** → Logs innovation audits.
- **Resilience-Dashboard.md** → Logs fallback activations.
- **Governance-Binder.md** → Consolidates innovation evidence.
- **Investor-Packet.md** → Publishes innovation disclosures.

Outcome

Provides a structured innovation agent, proving PayMate AI evolves systematically while maintaining governance and investor confidence.
