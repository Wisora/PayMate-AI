Purpose
Track usage, retention, and adoption metrics.  
Provide transparent evidence for governance, contributors, and investors.

Categories

- **Usage Metrics** → Monthly Active Users (MAU), Daily Active Users (DAU).
- **Retention Metrics** → 30-day, 90-day retention rates.
- **Churn Metrics** → Percentage of users leaving.
- **Adoption Growth** → New signups, market expansion, multilingual adoption.

Workflow

1. Metrics collected via Metrics-Agent.md and CI/CD pipeline.
2. Logged in Adoption-Dashboard.md with type, value, and evidence source.
3. Linked to Governance Binder and Quarterly-Confidence-Summary.md.
4. Consolidated quarterly for investor disclosure in Investor-Packet.md.
5. Reviewed annually for board deck presentation.

Evidence Flow

- **Metrics-Agent.md** → Collects adoption data.
- **Governance-Binder.md** → Consolidates adoption evidence.
- **Quarterly-Confidence-Summary.md** → Confidence impact logged.
- **Investor-Packet.md** → Publishes adoption disclosures.

Outcome

Provides transparent adoption evidence, proving PayMate AI tracks growth, retention, and churn systematically for investor confidence.
