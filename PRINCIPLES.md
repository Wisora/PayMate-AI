# Principles – PayMate AI

Purpose
Define the operational principles that guide contributors, governance leads, and investors.  
Provides a practical framework for collaboration, compliance, and accountability.

Governance Principles

- **Cadence Discipline** → Quarterly reviews and annual consolidation are non‑negotiable.
- **Transparency** → All risks, audits, and changes logged in registers.
- **Accountability** → Clear ownership assigned for every action and decision.
- **Evidence‑Driven** → Governance decisions backed by audit logs and metrics.

Compliance Principles

- **Accessibility First** → WCAG 2.1 AA compliance enforced across all assets.
- **Multilingual Inclusivity** → English, Spanish, French minimum; expansion roadmap required.
- **Continuous Monitoring** → Compliance checks integrated into CI/CD pipeline.
- **Regulatory Alignment** → GDPR and financial regulations adhered to at all times.

Product Principles

- **Daily Utility** → Features must deliver tangible everyday value (fraud alerts, subscription tracking).
- **Scalability** → Cost‑efficient growth from free tiers to enterprise adoption.
- **Documentation Integrity** → Repo maps, onboarding guides, and contributor standards maintained.
- **Security by Design** → Vulnerability scans and patch cycles embedded in workflows.

Investor Principles

- **Investor‑Ready Repo** → Governance suite and registers always up to date.
- **Localized Communication** → Decks and reports available in English, Spanish, French.
- **Evidence Transparency** → Metrics, audits, and lessons learned shared openly.
- **Confidence Building** → Continuous improvement cycle demonstrated in board presentations.

Review Cadence

- **Quarterly** → Principles reaffirmed in governance binder.
- **Annual** → Principles presented in board deck alongside vision, values, and strategy.
