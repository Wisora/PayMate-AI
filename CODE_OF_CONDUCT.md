# Code of Conduct – PayMate AI

Purpose
Establish community standards for contributors, ensuring professionalism, inclusivity, and investor‑ready governance.

Our Standards

We commit to a welcoming and respectful environment. Examples of positive behavior include:

- Using inclusive language.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community.
- Demonstrating empathy toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, or offensive comments.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Disruptive or unprofessional conduct in discussions or commits.
- Ignoring governance, compliance, or security standards.

Responsibilities

- **Governance Lead** → Enforces governance cadence and binder integrity.
- **Compliance Officer** → Ensures accessibility, security, and regulatory compliance.
- **Investor Relations Lead** → Maintains professional investor communications.
- **All Contributors** → Follow CONTRIBUTING.md, commitlint rules, and markdownlint standards.

Enforcement

- Reports of misconduct should be sent privately to: **<conduct@paymateai.com>**.
- All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly.
- Enforcement actions may include warnings, PR rejections, or removal from contributor lists.
- Governance Lead and Compliance Officer oversee enforcement decisions.

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- Repo contributions (commits, PRs, issues).
- Documentation and governance artifacts.
- Community spaces (discussions, investor presentations).
