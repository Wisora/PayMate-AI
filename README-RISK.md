# Risk Governance Overview – PayMate AI

Purpose
Provide board members, investors, and governance leads with a clear overview of risk governance.  
This README highlights the risk register, matrix, heatmap, calendar, and mitigation framework, ensuring transparency and board‑readiness.

Risk Suite

Core Files

- **[RISK-REGISTER.md](RISK-REGISTER.md)** → Evidence log of identified risks.
- **[RISK-MATRIX.md](RISK-MATRIX.md)** → Risk categorization by likelihood and impact.
- **[RISK-HEATMAP.md](RISK-HEATMAP.md)** → Visual representation of risk severity.
- **[RISK-CALENDAR.md](RISK-CALENDAR.md)** → Cadence of risk reviews and updates.

Mitigation Framework

- **[MITIGATION-REGISTER.md](MITIGATION-REGISTER.md)** → Evidence log of mitigation strategies linked to risks.
- **[MITIGATION-CALENDAR.md](MITIGATION-CALENDAR.md)** → Cadence of mitigation reviews and updates.

Risk Governance Principles

1. **Identification**
   - Risks logged in RISK-REGISTER.md.
   - Categorized in RISK-MATRIX.md by likelihood and impact.

2. **Visualization**
   - Risks mapped in RISK-HEATMAP.md for board visibility.
   - Updated quarterly in RISK-CALENDAR.md.

3. **Mitigation**
   - Linked strategies documented in MITIGATION-REGISTER.md.
   - Reviewed quarterly in MITIGATION-CALENDAR.md.

4. **Transparency**
   - Risks and mitigations consolidated annually in board deck.
   - Evidence integrated into GOVERNANCE-BINDER.md.

Workflow Integration

- Risks identified → logged in register.
- Risks categorized → matrix and heatmap.
- Risks mitigated → mitigation register and calendar.
- Evidence consolidated → governance binder and board deck.

Usage

- **Board Members** → Review risk suite for confidence in governance maturity.
- **Investors** → Assess risk transparency and mitigation cadence.
- **Governance Leads** → Ensure risks and mitigations logged and escalated.

Review Cadence

- **Quarterly** → Risk and mitigation calendars updated with evidence.
- **Annual** → Consolidated risk governance presented in board deck.
