Compliance Governance Principles

1. **Standards**
   - Compliance requirements logged in COMPLIANCE-REGISTER.md.
   - Linked to audit and quality domains for traceability.

2. **Cadence**
   - Quarterly compliance reviews tracked in COMPLIANCE-CALENDAR.md.
   - Annual consolidated compliance audit presented in board deck.

3. **Evidence Logging**
   - Compliance outcomes documented in registers.
   - Integrated into governance binder for board confidence.

4. **Transparency**
   - Compliance evidence consolidated annually.
   - Investor disclosures aligned with compliance cadence.
