# Board Register – PayMate AI

Purpose
Log board escalations, decisions, and sign‑offs.  
Provide transparent governance evidence for contributors, investors, and regulators.

Structure

| Board ID | Date | Escalation / Decision | Description | Impact | Linked Governance ID | Linked Change ID | Owner Role | Status |

|----------|------|------------------------|-------------|--------|----------------------|------------------|------------|--------|

| BR-001 | 2026-06-15 | Escalation | Database outage escalated to board | High | Governance-Binder.md → GB-010 | Change-Register.md → CR-015 | Governance Lead | Resolved |
| BR-002 | 2026-06-20 | Decision | Approve SOC 2 audit disclosure | Medium | Governance-Binder.md → GB-011 | Change-Register.md → CR-016 | Compliance Officer | Approved |
| BR-003 | 2026-06-21 | Sign‑Off | Quarterly release confidence summary | High | Governance-Binder.md → GB-012 | Change-Register.md → CR-017 | Board Chair | Signed |

Categories

- **Escalations** → Risks or issues requiring board attention.
- **Decisions** → Board approvals on compliance, scaling, or investor disclosures.
- **Sign‑Offs** → Quarterly and annual governance validations.

Workflow

1. Escalation or decision raised to board.
2. Logged in Board-Register.md with description and impact.
3. Linked to Governance Binder and Change-Register.md.
4. Status updated (Resolved, Approved, Signed).
5. Reviewed quarterly in Governance Binder and disclosed in Investor-Packet.md.

Evidence Flow

- **Governance-Binder.md** → Consolidates board escalations and sign‑offs.
- **Change-Register.md** → Tracks systemic changes approved by board.
- **Quarterly-Confidence-Summary.md** → Publishes board‑validated confidence trends.
- **Investor-Packet.md** → Discloses board decisions to investors.

Outcome

Provides transparent board governance evidence, proving PayMate AI’s oversight is structured, auditable, and investor‑ready.
