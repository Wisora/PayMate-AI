# PayMate AI – Investor Governance Audit Evidence Handbook

Purpose
Provide contributors, board members, and investors with a unified handbook.  
Bundles policy, procedures, checklist, template, workflow, and glossary into one guide.

Reference: `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-policy.md`  
Defines standards, responsibilities, and retention rules for evidence.  
Key Principle: Evidence must be accurate, traceable, consistent, and transparent.

Section 2 – Procedures

Reference: `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-procedures.md`  
Step‑by‑step operational manual for collection, verification, storage, and reporting.  
Key Principle: Evidence lifecycle is structured and repeatable.

Section 3 – Checklist

Reference: `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-checklist.md`

Section 4 – Template

Reference: `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-template.md`  
Reusable submission form for standardized evidence entries.  
Key Principle: Metadata (ID, type, control, KPI, owner) captured consistently.

Section 5 – Workflow

Reference: `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-workflow.md`  
Visualized lifecycle: Collection → Verification → Storage → Reporting → Transparency.  
Key Principle: Evidence flows seamlessly through governance system.

Section 6 – Glossary

Reference: `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-glossary.md`  
Defines terms, acronyms, and categories for shared understanding.  
Key Principle: Common language across contributors and investors.

Section 7 – Companion Files
