# PayMate AI – Governance Dashboard Training Module

Purpose
Provide contributors with a structured training curriculum for operating and maintaining the governance dashboard.  
Ensures consistency, accountability, and investor confidence in dashboard workflows.

Training Objectives

- Understand dashboard layout and components.
- Learn how to update KPI tiles and charts.
- Practice evidence submission and verification workflows.
- Apply accessibility and performance testing.
- Prepare investor‑ready summaries and packet exports.

Module Outline

Lesson 1 – Dashboard Orientation

- Walkthrough of KPI tiles, charts, evidence snapshots, and investor summary.
- Exercise: Identify each component and its purpose.

Lesson 2 – Evidence Submission

- Review evidence templates and directory structure.
- Exercise: Submit sample compliance evidence item.
- Role‑play: Contributor submits evidence, Governance Lead reviews.

Lesson 3 – KPI Updates

- Learn how KPI values are refreshed from metrics file.
- Exercise: Update KPI tile with new quarterly data.
- Role‑play: Governance Lead validates KPI accuracy.

Lesson 4 – Accessibility & Performance

- Review accessibility checklist and testing guide.
- Exercise: Run Lighthouse and axe scans.
- Role‑play: Compliance Officer validates accessibility compliance.

Lesson 5 – Investor Packet Alignment

- Learn how dashboard visuals integrate into investor packets.
- Exercise: Export dashboard visuals to quarterly summary.
- Role‑play: Investor Relations Lead reviews packet alignment.

Lesson 6 – Release Workflow

- Review release checklist and sign‑off process.
- Exercise: Complete mock release checklist.
- Role‑play: Team signs off dashboard release.

Assessment

- Quiz on glossary, categories, and workflows.
- Practical exercise: Update dashboard with sample data.
- Role‑play scenario: Conduct quarterly release simulation.
- Passing criteria: ≥ 90% accuracy in KPI updates and checklist completion.

Certification

- Successful completion grants **Dashboard Contributor Certification**.
- Certification logged in evidence register.
- Renewal required annually with refresher training.
