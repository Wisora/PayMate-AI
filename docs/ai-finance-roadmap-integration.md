# PayMate AI – Roadmap Integration Guide

Purpose
Provide a structured template for integrating lessons learned into the roadmap.  
Ensures continuous improvement and investor transparency by showing how insights shape future milestones.

Inputs

- Quarterly Verification outcomes (`docs/quarterly-verification.md`)
- Annual Verification outcomes (`docs/annual-verification.md`)
- Final Review outcomes (`docs/ai-finance-final-review.md`)
- Lessons Learned log (`docs/ai-finance-lessons-learned.md`)

Integration Steps

1. **Collect Insights**
   - Gather successes, challenges, and improvement opportunities from lessons learned.
   - Confirm alignment with quarterly and annual verification outcomes.

2. **Prioritize Improvements**
   - Categorize into Must‑Have, Nice‑to‑Have, and Future Expansion.
   - Assign ownership to contributors or governance leads.

3. **Update Roadmap**
   - Add new milestones reflecting lessons learned.
   - Adjust timelines based on annual outcomes.
   - Archive updated roadmap visuals in `/docs/images/roadmap-diagram.png`.

4. **Investor Alignment**
   - Include roadmap updates in investor packet.
   - Highlight improvements driven by lessons learned.
   - Confirm accessibility and compliance standards applied.

Roadmap Integration Checklist

- [ ] Lessons learned collected from logs
- [ ] Improvements categorized (Must‑Have / Nice‑to‑Have / Future Expansion)
- [ ] Roadmap milestones updated
- [ ] Roadmap visuals refreshed
- [ ] Investor packet aligned with roadmap updates
- [ ] Accessibility and compliance confirmed
