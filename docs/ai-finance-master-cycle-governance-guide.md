# PayMate AI – Governance Guide for Cycles

Purpose
Provide a dedicated governance guide.  
Ensures board oversight, compliance alignment, and investor confidence across quarterly, annual, and final stages.

Governance Standards

- [ ] Governance framework documented in `/docs/governance/framework.md`
- [ ] Board oversight confirmed across compliance, security, quality, and risk cycles
- [ ] Governance visuals prepared for investor packets
- [ ] Governance logs maintained in `/logs/governance/`
- [ ] Governance ↔ Investor sync validated

Quarterly Governance Cycle

- [ ] Quarterly governance review completed
- [ ] Board oversight confirmed + logged
- [ ] Governance visuals refreshed + archived
- [ ] Quarterly governance report archived in `/logs/governance/quarterly/`

Annual Governance Cycle

- [ ] Annual governance review completed + archived
- [ ] Governance packet aligned with investor annual packet
- [ ] Annual governance visuals updated + localized for multilingual support
- [ ] Annual governance packet archived in `/logs/governance/annual/`

Final Governance Review

- [ ] Governance checklist confirmed in master final review
- [ ] Governance visuals included in investor packet
- [ ] Infographic summary prepared for investor materials
- [ ] Accessibility standards applied across governance reports

Continuous Governance Loop

- [ ] Quarterly governance logs synced with annual governance report
- [ ] Annual governance report synced with master annual report
- [ ] Final governance review outcomes fed into next year’s roadmap
- [ ] Lessons learned documented + integrated into contributor workflows
