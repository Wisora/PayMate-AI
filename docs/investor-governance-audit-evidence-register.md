Purpose
Maintain a repository of governance audit evidence.  
Indexes reports, logs, screenshots, and sign‑offs linked to controls and KPIs for transparency.

Usage Notes

- Evidence stored in `/evidence/` directory with subfolders by dimension.
- Each evidence item linked to control ID and KPI for traceability.
- Evidence reviewed quarterly during governance audit cycles.
- Archived evidence retained for 3 years for investor transparency.
