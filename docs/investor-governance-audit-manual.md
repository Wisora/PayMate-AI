# PayMate AI – Investor Governance Audit Manual

Purpose
Provide a complete training manual for contributors conducting governance audits.  
Combines methodology, guidelines, templates, checklists, and examples into one reference.

Section 1 – Audit Methodology

- Define scope (compliance, security, accessibility, investor transparency)
- Collect evidence (docs, dashboards, verification logs, visuals)
- Execute audit (checks, scans, reviews)
- Document findings (minor, major, critical)
- Track remediation (tasks, deadlines, owners)
- Collect sign‑offs (Governance Lead, Compliance Officer, Investor Relations, Finance)
- Close audit and archive

Section 2 – Evidence Standards

- Documentation updated within last 12 months
- Dashboards reflect latest quarterly cycle
- Accessibility evidence meets WCAG 2.1 AA standards
- Security evidence includes vulnerability scans and patch rollout logs
- Investor transparency evidence includes updated packets and archives

Section 6 – Audit Examples

- **Scope:** SOC 2, GDPR, accessibility review
- **Findings:** Minor GDPR gap, contrast issue in visuals

- **Status:** Closed

Example: Annual Audit (2025)

- **Sign‑Off:** GL ✅ | CO ✅ | IRL ✅ | FL ✅

Section 7 – Integration

- Log audits in `/docs/investor-governance-audit-log.md`
- Follow schedule in `/docs/investor-governance-audit-schedule.md`
- Use risk register `/docs/investor-governance-risk-register.md` and risk matrix `/docs/investor-governance-risk-matrix.md` for findings
- Sync with governance dashboards and metrics

Section 8 – Training Notes

- New contributors must complete onboarding and training before conducting audits
- Manual should be reviewed annually for relevance
- Deviations from methodology must be documented and approved by Governance Lead
