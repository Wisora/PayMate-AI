# PayMate AI – Investor Presentation Binder Governance Policy

Purpose
Define binding rules for the maintenance, compliance, and investor communication standards of the Investor Presentation Binder.  
Ensure the binder remains a trusted governance instrument, consistently investor‑ready and auditable.

Policy Statements

1. **Mandatory Maintenance**
   - Binder must be updated at the close of every fiscal quarter.
   - Mid‑cycle updates required if material governance changes occur (metrics, risks, certifications).

2. **Compliance Standards**
   - All binder materials must meet accessibility requirements (contrast, font size, alt text).
   - Certifications and compliance records must be current and logged in binder evidence chain.
   - Audit trail must capture every binder action with timestamps.

3. **Investor Communication Standards**
   - Executive Brief and Summary must be updated quarterly for investor readability.
   - Binder must include a complete evidence chain linking presentation claims to governance documentation.
   - Binder distributed to investors only after formal approval register sign‑off.

4. **Governance Accountability**
   - Governance Lead responsible for completeness and evidence validation.
   - Compliance Officer responsible for accessibility and certification compliance.
   - Investor Relations Lead responsible for alignment with investor packet cadence.
   - Finance Lead responsible for financial accuracy and quarterly sign‑offs.

Enforcement

- Non‑compliance with binder policy must be logged in the audit trail and escalated to Governance Lead.
- Binder governance charter and policy reviewed annually for alignment with evolving investor requirements.
- Policy stored in `/docs/investor-presentations/binder-governance-policy.md`.

Usage Notes

- Policy applies to all contributors and leads involved in binder maintenance.
- Serves as binding governance framework for investor communication.
- Complements binder charter, orientation, checklist, review log, approval register, and audit trail.
