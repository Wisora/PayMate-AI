# PayMate AI – Investor Presentation Binder Audit Variance Log

Purpose
Record variances between audit targets and actuals, document causes, and track corrective actions.  
Provides auditors and governance leads with transparency into deviations and remediation efforts.

Audit Variance Log Format

| Variance ID | Quarter | Date | Metric Category | Target | Actual | Variance | Cause | Corrective Action | Owner Role | Status |

|-------------|---------|------------|-----------------------|--------|--------|----------|-------|------------------|-------------------|--------|

| AVL‑001 | Q1 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Accessibility Checks | 100% | 98% | -2% | Missing alt text in visuals | Introduced accessibility checklist | Compliance Officer | Resolved |
| AVL‑002 | Q2 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Certification Renewals| 100% | 95% | -5% | Renewal reminders delayed | Automated certification alerts | Governance Lead | Resolved |
| AVL‑003 | Q3 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Audit Trail Completeness | 100% | 90% | -10% | Manual logging incomplete | Implemented automated logging | Investor Relations | In Progress |
| AVL‑004 | Q4 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Governance Alignment | 100% | 97% | -3% | Risk register misaligned | Scheduled mid‑cycle governance sync | Finance Lead | Completed |

Variance Categories

1. **Accessibility Variances** → Deviations in compliance with accessibility standards.
2. **Certification Variances** → Gaps in certification renewals or evidence logging.
3. **Audit Trail Variances** → Missing or incomplete lifecycle entries.
4. **Governance Variances** → Misalignment between binder and governance records.

Workflow

- Variances identified during quarterly audit reviews.
- Causes documented with context.
- Corrective actions assigned to owner roles.
- Status tracked until resolution.
- Variance log updated quarterly alongside audit metrics register.

Usage Notes

- Stored in `/docs/investor-presentations/binder-audit-variance-log.md`.
- Serves as **variance tracking evidence** for auditors and investors.
- Complements audit metrics register, audit dashboard, and audit register.
