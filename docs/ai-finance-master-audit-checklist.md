# PayMate AI – Master Audit Checklist

Purpose
Provide a consolidated audit checklist.  
Ensures internal and external audits across governance, security, compliance, and quality are fully tracked and aligned annually before investor reviews.

Governance Audits

- [ ] Internal governance audits logged quarterly
- [ ] Board oversight reviews documented
- [ ] Policy updates confirmed + archived in `/logs/governance/annual/`
- [ ] External governance reviews synced with investor packets

Security Audits

- [ ] SOC 2 audit results confirmed
- [ ] PCI‑DSS compliance maintained
- [ ] ISO 27001 roadmap progress logged
- [ ] External audit findings archived in `/logs/security/annual/`

Compliance Audits

- [ ] Certification renewals verified (PCI‑DSS, SOC 2, ISO)
- [ ] Regulatory updates confirmed (GDPR, POPIA, CCPA)
- [ ] Internal compliance audits logged
- [ ] External compliance audits archived in `/logs/compliance/annual/`

Quality Audits

- [ ] Internal quality audits logged quarterly
- [ ] External vendor audits confirmed
- [ ] Audit trail archived in `/logs/quality/annual/`
- [ ] Findings integrated into roadmap + contributor workflows

Transparency Layer

- [ ] Annual audit outcomes consolidated into master annual report
- [ ] Infographic summaries included in investor packets
- [ ] Accessibility standards applied (alt text, multilingual captions, screen reader compatibility)
- [ ] Audit logs synced with governance, security, compliance, and quality annual reports
