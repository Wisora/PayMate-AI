# PayMate AI – Investor Presentation Deck Outline

Purpose
Provide a structured slide‑by‑slide outline for quarterly investor presentations.  
Ensures consistent, polished delivery of governance, compliance, and roadmap updates.

Deck Structure

Slide 1 – Title & Executive Overview

- Company logo & dashboard branding
- Quarter, release version, date
- Key highlights (1–2 bullet points)

Slide 2 – KPI Performance Snapshot

- Table of KPI targets vs actuals
- Trend chart visual (from metrics trend chart)
- Commentary on improvements or anomalies

Slide 3 – Risks & Mitigation

- Top 3 risks identified this quarter
- Mitigation actions in progress
- Link to risk register

Slide 4 – Continuous Improvement

- Key enhancements delivered
- Lessons learned
- Link to continuous improvement log

Slide 5 – Roadmap & Maturity Model

- Current maturity stage
- Roadmap progression (next quarter milestones)
- Long‑term alignment with investor goals

Slide 6 – Audit Evidence & Certifications

- Snapshot of audit evidence log entries
- Certification register highlights
- Compliance and accessibility verification status

Slide 7 – Investor Engagement

- Feedback log summary
- Q&A module highlights
- Communication cadence (calendar + cadence diagram)

Slide 8 – Financial & Governance Sign‑Off

- Finance lead commentary
- Governance lead sign‑off statement
- Compliance officer assurance

Slide 9 – Closing Notes

- Reinforce transparency commitment
- Confirm next quarterly meeting date
- Contact details for investor relations

Usage Notes

- Deck prepared quarterly alongside investor packet.
- Visuals pulled from metrics dashboard, trend chart, roadmap, and cadence diagram.
- Stored in `/docs/investor-presentations/`.
- Serves as both investor communication and audit evidence.
