# PayMate AI – Investor Presentation Binder Mitigation Register

Purpose
Document planned mitigation strategies for recurring risks identified in binder compliance reviews.  
Provides a forward‑looking governance tool to ensure risks are managed proactively across quarters.

Mitigation Register Format

| Mitigation ID | Quarter | Risk Category | Risk Description | Planned Mitigation Strategy | Owner Role | Target Date | Status |

|---------------|---------|---------------------|------------------|-----------------------------|---------------------|-------------|--------|

| MR‑001 | Q1 YYYY | Accessibility | Visuals missing alt text | Implement accessibility checklist for all visuals | Compliance Officer | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Planned |
| MR‑002 | Q2 YYYY | Certification | Certification renewals delayed | Automate certification renewal reminders | Governance Lead | YYYY‑MM‑DD | In Progress |
| MR‑003 | Q3 YYYY | Audit Trail | Incomplete log entries | Introduce automated audit trail logging | Investor Relations | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Planned |
| MR‑004 | Q4 YYYY | Governance Records | Risk register misaligned | Schedule mid‑cycle governance sync | Finance Lead | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Planned |

Risk Categories

1. **Accessibility Risks** → Ensure visuals and documents meet accessibility standards.
2. **Certification Risks** → Prevent lapses in certification renewals.
3. **Audit Risks** → Guarantee completeness of audit trail entries.
4. **Governance Risks** → Maintain alignment between binder and governance records.

Workflow

- Risks identified in **Risk Compliance Log**.
- Mitigation strategies documented in **Mitigation Register**.
- Owner role assigned to ensure accountability.
- Target dates tracked until mitigation completed.
- Register updated quarterly alongside compliance register and audit trail.

Usage Notes

- Stored in `/docs/investor-presentations/binder-mitigation-register.md`.
- Serves as proactive governance evidence for investors and auditors.
- Complements binder compliance register, risk compliance log, and audit trail.
