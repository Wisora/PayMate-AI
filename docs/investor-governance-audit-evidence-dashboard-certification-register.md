# PayMate AI – Governance Dashboard Certification Register

Purpose
Maintain a structured record of certifications, renewals, and compliance attestations linked to dashboard KPIs.  
Provides transparency and assurance for auditors, contributors, and investors.

Certification Register Format

| Cert ID | Date Issued | Certification Type | Scope | Linked KPI | Renewal Date | Owner | Status |

|---------|-------------|--------------------|-------|------------|--------------|-------|--------|

| C‑001 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA) | Dashboard UI | Accessibility Rate | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Compliance Officer | Active |
| C‑002 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Security Audit (ISO 27001) | Infrastructure | Security Rate | YYYY‑MM‑DD | QA Lead | Active |
| C‑003 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Compliance Attestation | Regulatory Reporting | Compliance Rate | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Governance Lead | Active |
| C‑004 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Investor Transparency Certification | Investor Packet | Transparency Rate | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Investor Relations Lead | Active |

Certification Types

- **Accessibility:** WCAG compliance, usability certifications.
- **Security:** ISO audits, penetration test certifications.
- **Compliance:** Regulatory attestations, compliance audits.
- **Verification:** Certification records, validation workflows.
- **Transparency:** Investor packet certifications, disclosure attestations.

Usage Notes

- Certifications logged immediately upon issuance.
- Renewal dates tracked to ensure continuous compliance.
- Status tracked as _Active_, _Expired_, _Renewal Pending_.
- Reviewed quarterly during governance meetings.
- Serves as audit evidence for external reviewers and investors.
