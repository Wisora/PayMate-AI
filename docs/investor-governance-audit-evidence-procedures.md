# PayMate AI – Investor Governance Audit Evidence Procedures

Purpose
Provide a step‑by‑step operational manual for governance audit evidence.  
Defines how evidence is collected, verified, stored, and reported in practice.

Evidence Collection

1. **Trigger:** Evidence generated during governance activities (e.g., audits, scans, sign‑offs).
2. **Capture:** Collect raw files (logs, reports, screenshots, packets).
3. **Format:** Standardize into approved formats (PDF, CSV, PNG).
4. **Tag:** Assign Evidence ID, Control ID, and KPI linkage.
5. **Store:** Place in `/evidence/` directory under correct category.

Evidence Verification

1. **Review:** Owner checks completeness and accuracy.
2. **Cross‑Check:** Link evidence to relevant control and KPI.
3. **Approval:** Governance Lead or Compliance Officer signs off.
4. **Escalation:** Any anomalies flagged in risk log.

Evidence Storage

1. **Directory Structure:** `/evidence/[dimension]/[type]/[year‑quarter]/`
2. **Access Control:** Read‑only for contributors, write access for owners.
3. **Retention:** Minimum 3 years (5 years for investor packets).
4. **Archiving:** Annual archive review and secure backup.

Evidence Reporting

1. **Register Update:** Add new evidence to `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-register.md`.
2. **Index Update:** Update `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-index.md` with links.
3. **Summary Digest:** Curate highlights in `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-summary.md`.
4. **Dashboard Integration:** Visualize KPIs and trends in `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-dashboard.md`.
5. **Investor Packets:** Include curated evidence highlights quarterly.

Review & Continuous Improvement

- Procedures reviewed annually alongside evidence policy.
- Improvements logged in action register and roadmap.
- Benchmarking against industry standards for maturity progression.
