# PayMate AI – Investor Governance Risk Matrix

Purpose
Provide a visual/text matrix mapping governance risks by likelihood vs impact.  
Helps investors quickly assess severity and prioritization.

Risk Matrix Table

| Impact \ Likelihood | Low Likelihood | Medium Likelihood | High Likelihood |
| ------------------- | -------------- | ----------------- | --------------- |

| **Low Impact** | Monitor only | Track quarterly | Escalate if repeated |
| **Medium Impact** | Track quarterly| Mitigation required | Escalate to Governance Lead |
| **High Impact** | Mitigation required | Escalate immediately | Critical risk – investor disclosure |

Example Risks Plotted

- **R‑2026‑01 SOC 2 remediation delay** → Medium Likelihood / High Impact → Escalate immediately
- **R‑2026‑02 Patch rollout exceeds SLA** → Low Likelihood / High Impact → Mitigation required
- **R‑2026‑03 Accessibility audit fails WCAG** → Medium Likelihood / Medium Impact → Mitigation required
- **R‑2026‑04 Quarterly verification missed** → Low Likelihood / High Impact → Mitigation required
- **R‑2026‑05 Investor dashboard update delayed** → Medium Likelihood / Medium Impact → Mitigation required
- **R‑2026‑07 Finance misalignment** → Low Likelihood / High Impact → Escalate immediately

Usage Notes

- Update matrix **quarterly** alongside risk register.
- Use matrix in investor packets to visualize risk severity.
- Critical risks (High Impact + High Likelihood) must be disclosed to investors.
