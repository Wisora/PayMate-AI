# PayMate AI – Investor Presentation Binder Orientation

Purpose
Provide contributors with clear guidance on binder responsibilities, workflows, and update cadence.  
Ensures the binder remains complete, accurate, and investor‑ready each quarter.

Contributor Responsibilities

- **Governance Lead:** Oversees binder completeness, validates evidence chain, signs approvals.
- **Compliance Officer:** Ensures accessibility, compliance, and certification records are updated.
- **Investor Relations Lead:** Aligns binder with investor packet cadence and communication standards.
- **Finance Lead:** Confirms financial accuracy and quarterly sign‑offs.
- **Contributors:** Maintain supporting docs (metrics, risk register, visuals) and update binder links.

Workflow

1. **Preparation Phase**
   - Update deck, style guide, template, and checklist.
   - Refresh governance evidence (metrics, risk register, certifications).

2. **Review Phase**
   - Governance Lead validates completeness.
   - Compliance Officer checks accessibility and compliance.
   - Investor Relations Lead ensures alignment with investor packet.
   - Finance Lead confirms financial commentary.

3. **Approval Phase**
   - Review Log updated with comments.
   - Approval Register signed by all leads.
   - Audit Trail logged with timestamps.

4. **Delivery Phase**
   - Binder finalized and stored in `/docs/investor-presentations/audit-evidence-binder.md`.
   - PDF version exported for investor packet.
   - Backup archived in repo.

Update Cadence

- **Quarterly Updates:** Binder refreshed at the end of each fiscal quarter.
- **Mid‑Cycle Updates:** Binder updated if major governance changes occur (metrics, risks, certifications).
- **Annual Review:** Binder orientation and workflows reviewed for improvements.

Usage Notes

- Orientation document stored in `/docs/investor-presentations/binder-orientation.md`.
- Serves as the **internal guide** for contributors maintaining the binder.
- Ensures consistency, accountability, and audit readiness across all cycles.
