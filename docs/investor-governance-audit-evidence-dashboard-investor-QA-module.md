# PayMate AI – Governance Dashboard Investor Q&A Module

Purpose
Provide a structured framework for capturing, categorizing, and responding to investor questions.  
Ensures transparency, accountability, and consistency in investor communications.

Q&A Workflow

1. **Capture**
   - Record investor questions during meetings, emails, or Q&A sessions.
   - Assign a unique Question ID.

2. **Categorize**
   - Accessibility, Performance, Data Accuracy, Transparency, Governance Workflow.
   - Tag questions with priority (Low / Medium / High).

3. **Respond**
   - Draft clear, factual responses.
   - Link to supporting evidence (audit log, metrics file, roadmap).
   - Assign owner for follow‑up.

4. **Document**
   - Log in `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-dashboard-investor-feedback-log.md`.
   - Track resolution status.

5. **Follow‑Up**
   - Share resolution updates in next investor communication.
   - Confirm closure with investor.

Q&A Log Format

| Question ID | Date | Investor | Category | Question | Priority | Response | Owner | Status |

|-------------|------------|----------|-----------------|----------|----------|----------|-------|--------|

| Q‑001 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Investor A | Accessibility | How are charts accessible to screen readers? | Medium | Added ARIA labels and alt‑text | Compliance Officer | Completed |
| Q‑002 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Investor B | Transparency | Can KPI updates be shared monthly? | High | Increased update frequency | Governance Lead | In Progress |
| Q‑003 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Investor C | Performance | Why does mobile load time vary? | Medium | Optimized caching and queries | QA Lead | Completed |

Categories

- **Accessibility:** Inclusive design, WCAG compliance.
- **Performance:** Load times, responsiveness, optimization.
- **Data Accuracy:** KPI validation, evidence snapshots.
- **Transparency:** Frequency of updates, investor packet alignment.
- **Governance Workflow:** Roles, sign‑offs, release cadence.

Usage Notes

- Q&A module used during quarterly investor meetings and reviews.
- Questions logged immediately and tracked until resolution.
- Serves as audit evidence of investor engagement.
