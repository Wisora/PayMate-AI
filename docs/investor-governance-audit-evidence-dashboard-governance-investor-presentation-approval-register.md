# PayMate AI – Investor Presentation Approval Register

Purpose
Maintain a formal record of final sign‑offs for each quarterly investor presentation.  
Ensures accountability, transparency, and audit evidence of governance approvals.

Approval Register Format

| Approval ID | Quarter | Date | Deck Version | Governance Lead | Compliance Officer | Investor Relations Lead | Finance Lead | Status |

|-------------|---------|------------|--------------|-----------------|--------------------|-------------------------|--------------|--------|

| AP‑001 | Q1 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | v1.0 | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | Approved |
| AP‑002 | Q2 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | v1.1 | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | Approved |
| AP‑003 | Q3 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | v1.2 | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | Approved |
| AP‑004 | Q4 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | v1.3 | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | Approved |

Approval Workflow

1. **Deck Prepared** → Contributors finalize slides using template & checklist.
2. **Review Log Completed** → Comments and revisions tracked in review log.
3. **Final Sign‑Off** → Each lead signs approval register entry.
4. **Deck Delivered** → Approved deck included in investor packet.

Usage Notes

- Each quarterly deck must have a unique Approval ID.
- Status updated only when all leads have signed.
- Register stored in `/docs/investor-presentations/approval-register.md`.
- Serves as formal audit evidence of governance accountability.
