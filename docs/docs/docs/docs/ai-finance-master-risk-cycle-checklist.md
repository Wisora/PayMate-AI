# PayMate AI – Master Risk Cycle Checklist

Purpose
Provide a consolidated risk cycle checklist.  
Ensures incidents, mitigations, and risk assessments flow smoothly across quarterly, annual, and final stages before investor reviews.

Quarterly Risk

- [ ] Risk incidents logged in `/logs/risk/quarterly/`
- [ ] Risks categorized (operational / compliance / security / financial)
- [ ] Severity levels documented (low / medium / high)
- [ ] Mitigation strategies applied + tracked
- [ ] Quarterly risk review completed + archived

Annual Risk

- [ ] Annual risk assessment completed
- [ ] Risk scoring updated (likelihood × impact)
- [ ] Certification + compliance risks reviewed
- [ ] Annual risk report visual completed
- [ ] Findings archived in `/logs/risk/annual/`

Final Risk Review

- [ ] Risk checklist confirmed in master final review
- [ ] Risk visuals included in investor packet
- [ ] Infographic summary prepared for investor materials
- [ ] Accessibility standards applied (alt text, multilingual captions, screen reader compatibility)

Continuous Risk Loop

- [ ] Quarterly risk logs synced with annual risk report
- [ ] Annual risk report synced with master annual report
- [ ] Final risk review outcomes fed into next year’s risk roadmap
- [ ] Lessons learned documented + integrated into contributor workflows
