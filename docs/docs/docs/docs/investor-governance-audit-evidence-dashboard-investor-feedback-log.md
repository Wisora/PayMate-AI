# PayMate AI – Governance Dashboard Investor Feedback Log

Purpose
Capture and track investor comments, concerns, and requests related to the governance dashboard.  
Ensures transparency, accountability, and continuous improvement in investor communications.

Feedback Log Format

| Feedback ID | Date | Investor | Category | Feedback | Priority | Action Taken | Owner | Status |

|-------------|------------|----------|-----------------|----------|----------|--------------|-------|--------|

| F‑001 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Investor A | Accessibility | Requested clearer chart labels | Medium | Updated chart legends | Compliance Officer | Completed |
| F‑002 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Investor B | Transparency | Asked for more frequent KPI updates | High | Increased update frequency to monthly | Governance Lead | In Progress |
| F‑003 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Investor C | Performance | Concerned about mobile load times | Medium | Optimized mobile caching | QA Lead | Completed |
| F‑004 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Investor D | Data Accuracy | Requested evidence snapshots in packet | High | Added export step to release workflow | Investor Relations Lead | Completed |

Categories

- **Accessibility:** Clarity of visuals, inclusive design.
- **Transparency:** Frequency of updates, visibility of data.
- **Performance:** Load times, responsiveness, mobile optimization.
- **Data Accuracy:** KPI validation, evidence snapshots.
- **Governance Workflow:** Sign‑off clarity, release cadence.

Usage Notes

- Feedback logged immediately after investor communication.
- Priority rated Low / Medium / High.
- Action taken documented and tracked until resolution.
- Reviewed quarterly in governance meetings.
- Serves as proof of responsiveness for auditors and investors.
