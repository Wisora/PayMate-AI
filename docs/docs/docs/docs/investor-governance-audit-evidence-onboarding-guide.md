# PayMate AI – Investor Governance Audit Evidence Onboarding Guide

Purpose
Provide new contributors with a step‑by‑step onboarding manual.  
Ensures consistent use of the handbook, checklist, and template when handling governance evidence.

Step 1 – Orientation

- Read the **Evidence Handbook** (`/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-handbook.md`).
- Familiarize yourself with policy, procedures, workflow, and glossary.
- Understand the importance of evidence in investor transparency.

Step 2 – Evidence Standards

- Review the **Evidence Policy** for rules on accuracy, retention, and responsibilities.
- Learn the categories: Compliance, Accessibility, Security, Verification, Transparency.
- Understand retention rules (3 years minimum, 5 years for investor packets).

Step 3 – Submission Process

- Use the **Evidence Template** (`/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-template.md`) to log new items.
- Fill in Evidence ID, type, Control ID, KPI, description, location, and owner.
- Store evidence in the correct `/evidence/` subdirectory.

Step 4 – Verification

- Complete the **Evidence Checklist** (`/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-checklist.md`) before submission.
- Ensure accuracy, completeness, and linkage to controls/KPIs.
- Submit for Governance Lead or Compliance Officer sign‑off.

Step 5 – Workflow Familiarization

- Study the **Evidence Workflow** (`/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-workflow.md`).
- Understand the lifecycle: Collection → Verification → Storage → Reporting → Transparency.
- Know your role in each stage.

Step 6 – Glossary & Metrics

- Use the **Glossary** (`/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-glossary.md`) for shared terminology.
- Review **Metrics** (`/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-metrics.md`) to understand how KPIs are calculated.
- Check **Data Sources** (`/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-data-sources.md`) for traceability.

Step 7 – Continuous Improvement

- Participate in quarterly reviews of evidence practices.
- Suggest improvements logged in the governance roadmap.
- Align with maturity model progression (Stage 3 → Stage 4).

Usage Notes

- This guide is mandatory for all new contributors.
- Updated annually alongside the handbook and policy.
- Serves as the onboarding foundation for governance evidence practices.
