# PayMate AI – Risk Guide for Cycles

Purpose
Provide a dedicated risk guide.  
Ensures incident categorization, scoring, and mitigation strategies are consistently applied across quarterly, annual, and final stages.

Risk Standards

- [ ] Risk framework documented in `/docs/risk/framework.md`
- [ ] Incident categories defined (operational, financial, compliance, security, reputational)
- [ ] Risk scoring methodology applied (likelihood × impact)
- [ ] Mitigation strategies logged in `/logs/risk/mitigations/`
- [ ] Risk register maintained + updated quarterly

Quarterly Risk Cycle

- [ ] Quarterly risk assessment completed
- [ ] Incident categories reviewed + updated
- [ ] Mitigation strategies confirmed + logged
- [ ] Quarterly risk report archived in `/logs/risk/quarterly/`

Annual Risk Cycle

- [ ] Annual risk audit completed + archived
- [ ] Risk scoring recalibrated against industry benchmarks
- [ ] Annual risk register compiled
- [ ] Annual risk report archived in `/logs/risk/annual/`

Final Risk Review

- [ ] Risk checklist confirmed in master final review
- [ ] Risk visuals included in investor packet
- [ ] Infographic summary prepared for investor materials
- [ ] Accessibility standards applied across risk reports

Continuous Risk Loop

- [ ] Quarterly risk logs synced with annual risk report
- [ ] Annual risk report synced with master annual report
- [ ] Final risk review outcomes fed into next year’s roadmap
- [ ] Lessons learned documented + integrated into contributor workflows
