# PayMate AI – Investor Presentation Binder Governance Sign‑Off Register

Purpose
Record formal sign‑offs for each binder governance cycle.  
Ensures accountability and traceability across Governance, Compliance, Investor Relations, and Finance leads.

Sign‑Off Register Format

| Sign‑Off ID | Period | Date | Activity | Governance Lead | Compliance Officer | Investor Relations Lead | Finance Lead | Status |

|-------------|---------|------------|-----------|-----------------|--------------------|-------------------------|---------------|--------|

| SOR‑001 | Q1 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Compliance Review | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | Completed |
| SOR‑002 | Q2 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Audit Review | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | Completed |
| SOR‑003 | Q3 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Mid‑Cycle Governance Sync | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | Completed |
| SOR‑004 | Q4 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Annual Review & Audit Closing | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | Completed |

Sign‑Off Categories

1. **Quarterly Reviews** → Compliance, risk, and audit validations.
2. **Mid‑Cycle Syncs** → Governance alignment and binder updates.
3. **Annual Reviews** → Consolidation of evolution history and lessons learned.
4. **Audit Closings** → Final audit assurance and corrective action closure.

Workflow

- Each governance activity concludes with formal sign‑off by all four leads.
- Sign‑offs recorded in this register and referenced in the **Governance Calendar**.
- Register updated quarterly and archived annually.

Usage Notes

- Stored in `/docs/investor-presentations/binder-governance-sign-off-register.md`.
- Serves as **formal accountability evidence** for auditors and investors.
- Complements governance calendar, cadence diagram, and master index.

Annual Summary

- All quarterly and annual sign‑offs verified.
- Outstanding items flagged for next governance cycle.
- Binder governance assurance confirmed for investor reporting.
