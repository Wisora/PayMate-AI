# PayMate AI – Investor Presentation Binder Audit Register

Purpose
Maintain a detailed record of binder audit reviews, findings, and corrective actions.  
Serves as formal audit evidence for external auditors and internal governance assurance.

Audit Register Format

| Audit ID | Quarter | Date | Reviewer Role | Findings Summary | Corrective Actions | Status | Follow‑Up Date |

|----------|---------|------------|----------------------|-----------------|-------------------|--------|----------------|

| AR‑001 | Q1 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Compliance Officer | Accessibility gaps in visuals | Added alt text and updated contrast | Resolved | YYYY‑MM‑DD |
| AR‑002 | Q2 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Governance Lead | Certification renewal pending | Renewed certification and logged evidence | Resolved | YYYY‑MM‑DD |
| AR‑003 | Q3 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Investor Relations | Missing audit trail entries | Automated audit logging implemented | In Progress | YYYY‑MM‑DD |
| AR‑004 | Q4 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Finance Lead | Risk register misaligned | Mid‑cycle governance sync scheduled | Planned | YYYY‑MM‑DD |

Audit Categories

1. **Accessibility Audits** → Verify compliance with accessibility standards.
2. **Certification Audits** → Confirm certifications are current and logged.
3. **Audit Trail Audits** → Ensure completeness and timestamp accuracy of audit logs.
4. **Governance Audits** → Validate binder alignment with governance records.

Workflow

- Audit reviews conducted quarterly by governance leads.
- Findings documented in audit register with corrective actions.
- Status tracked as _Planned_, _In Progress_, or _Resolved_.
- Follow‑up dates scheduled to confirm corrective actions completed.

Usage Notes

- Audit register updated quarterly alongside compliance register and audit summary.
- Stored in `/docs/investor-presentations/binder-audit-register.md`.
- Serves as **formal audit trail** for external auditors and regulators.
