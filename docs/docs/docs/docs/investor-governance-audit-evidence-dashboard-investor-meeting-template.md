# PayMate AI – Governance Dashboard Investor Meeting Template

Purpose
Provide a standardized agenda and slide outline for quarterly investor meetings.  
Ensures consistent communication of governance dashboard updates, risks, and roadmap progression.

Meeting Agenda

1. **Welcome & Objectives**
   - Acknowledge investor trust and commitment to transparency.
   - State purpose of meeting (quarterly update, roadmap review, corrective actions).

2. **Dashboard Highlights**
   - KPI performance overview (Compliance, Accessibility, Security, Verification, Transparency).
   - Evidence snapshots summary.
   - Accessibility and performance improvements.

3. **Risks & Issues**
   - Top risks from risk register.
   - Mitigation actions and timelines.
   - Continuous improvement log highlights.

4. **Roadmap Progression**
   - Current maturity stage.
   - Planned enhancements for upcoming quarters.
   - Investor feedback integration.

5. **Investor Q&A**
   - Open floor for investor comments, concerns, and requests.
   - Capture feedback in `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-dashboard-investor-feedback-log.md`.

6. **Closing & Next Steps**
   - Confirm next communication milestone (quarterly release, roadmap update).
   - Reinforce commitment to governance maturity model.

Slide Deck Outline

Slide 1 – Title & Objectives

- Meeting title, date, and purpose.
- Governance principles (Transparency, Accountability, Accessibility).

Slide 2 – KPI Performance

- Charts for Compliance, Accessibility, Security, Verification, Transparency.
- Trend lines and quarterly comparisons.

Slide 3 – Evidence Snapshots

- Key audit evidence items.
- Accessibility and security highlights.

Slide 4 – Risks & Mitigation

- Risk register summary.
- Mitigation actions and owners.

Slide 5 – Roadmap Progression

- Current maturity stage.
- Planned enhancements (next 2 quarters).

Slide 6 – Continuous Improvement

- Lessons learned.
- Improvements logged.

Slide 7 – Investor Feedback

- Summary of recent investor comments.
- Actions taken and status.

Slide 8 – Q&A

- Placeholder for live discussion.

Slide 9 – Closing

- Next steps and communication milestones.
- Sign‑off roles.

Usage Notes

- Template reused for each quarterly investor meeting.
- Slide deck customized with current data and visuals.
- Feedback logged and tracked for continuous improvement.
