# PayMate AI – Investor Presentation Binder Risk Compliance Log

Purpose
Track risks identified during binder compliance reviews and document mitigation actions.  
Ensures transparency, accountability, and proactive management of compliance risks.

Risk Compliance Log Format

| Risk ID | Quarter | Date | Risk Description | Severity | Reviewer Role | Mitigation Action | Status |

|---------|---------|------------|------------------|----------|----------------------|------------------|--------|

| RC‑001 | Q1 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Accessibility gaps in visuals | Medium | Compliance Officer | Updated alt text and contrast | Resolved |
| RC‑002 | Q2 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Certification renewal pending | High | Governance Lead | Renewed certification and logged evidence | Resolved |
| RC‑003 | Q3 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Missing audit trail entries | High | Investor Relations | Completed audit log and verified timestamps | Resolved |
| RC‑004 | Q4 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Risk register not aligned with binder | Medium | Finance Lead | Updated risk register and binder links | Resolved |

Risk Categories

1. **Accessibility Risks** → Issues with compliance to accessibility standards.
2. **Certification Risks** → Expired or missing certifications in evidence chain.
3. **Audit Risks** → Incomplete or missing audit trail entries.
4. **Governance Risks** → Misalignment between binder and governance records.

Workflow

- Risks identified during quarterly compliance reviews.
- Severity rated as _Low_, _Medium_, or _High_.
- Mitigation actions documented and tracked until resolved.
- Risk Compliance Log updated and signed off each quarter.

Usage Notes

- Log updated quarterly alongside compliance register.
- Stored in `/docs/investor-presentations/binder-risk-compliance-log.md`.
- Serves as formal audit evidence of proactive risk management.
