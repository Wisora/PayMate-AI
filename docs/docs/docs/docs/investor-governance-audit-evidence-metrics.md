# PayMate AI – Investor Governance Audit Evidence Metrics

Purpose
Define KPIs, formulas, and data sources for governance audit evidence.  
Ensures consistency across registers, summaries, dashboards, and investor decks.

Compliance Metrics

- **Policy Currency Rate**  
  Formula: (# of policies reviewed annually ÷ total policies) × 100  
  Source: Policy review logs (E‑001)

- **Audit Completion Rate**  
  Formula: (# of completed audits ÷ scheduled audits) × 100  
  Source: Audit reports (E‑002)

Accessibility Metrics

- **WCAG Compliance Rate**  
  Formula: (# of compliant checkpoints ÷ total checkpoints) × 100  
  Source: Accessibility audit reports (E‑002)

- **Alt‑Text Coverage**  
  Formula: (# of images with alt‑text ÷ total images) × 100  
  Source: Contributor checklists (E‑003)

Security Metrics

- **Patch SLA Compliance**  
  Formula: (# of patches deployed within SLA ÷ total patches) × 100  
  Source: Deployment logs (E‑004)

- **Vulnerability Scan Coverage**  
  Formula: (# of systems scanned ÷ total systems) × 100  
  Source: Scan reports (E‑005)

- **Risk Register Update Rate**  
  Formula: (# of new findings logged ÷ total findings) × 100  
  Source: Risk register entries (E‑006)

Verification Metrics

- **Verification Completion Rate**  
  Formula: (# of governance cycles completed ÷ scheduled cycles) × 100  
  Source: Reminder logs (E‑007)

- **Contributor Accountability Rate**  
  Formula: (# of sign‑offs completed ÷ total required sign‑offs) × 100  
  Source: Sign‑off records (E‑008)

Transparency Metrics

- **Dashboard Sync Timeliness**  
  Formula: (# of dashboards updated within 7 days ÷ total dashboards) × 100  
  Source: Dashboard logs (E‑009)

- **Archive Completeness Rate**  
  Formula: (# of archived items ÷ total required items) × 100  
  Source: Archive index audits (E‑010)

- **Packet Alignment Rate**  
  Formula: (# of investor packets aligned ÷ total packets) × 100  
  Source: Investor packets (E‑011)

Usage Notes

- Metrics updated quarterly alongside evidence summary.
- Formulas standardized across dashboard and investor deck.
- Sources traceable to evidence register for transparency.
- KPIs benchmarked annually against governance maturity model.
