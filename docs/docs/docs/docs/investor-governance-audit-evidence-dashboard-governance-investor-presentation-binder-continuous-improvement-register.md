# PayMate AI – Investor Presentation Binder Continuous Improvement Register

Purpose
Capture lessons learned and process improvements applied to binder governance each quarter.  
Ensures continuous refinement of workflows, compliance, and investor communication standards.

Continuous Improvement Register Format

| Improvement ID | Quarter | Date | Area of Improvement | Lesson Learned | Action Taken | Owner Role | Status |

|----------------|---------|------------|---------------------|----------------|--------------|-------------------|--------|

| CI‑001 | Q1 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Accessibility | Alt text missing in visuals | Introduced accessibility checklist | Compliance Officer | Completed |
| CI‑002 | Q2 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Certification | Renewal reminders delayed | Automated certification alerts | Governance Lead | Completed |
| CI‑003 | Q3 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Audit Trail | Missing log entries | Implemented automated logging | Investor Relations | In Progress |
| CI‑004 | Q4 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Governance Records | Risk register misaligned | Scheduled mid‑cycle governance sync | Finance Lead | Completed |

Improvement Categories

1. **Accessibility** → Enhancements to ensure inclusive investor communication.
2. **Certification** → Improvements to certification renewal and evidence tracking.
3. **Audit Trail** → Strengthening completeness and automation of audit logs.
4. **Governance Records** → Better alignment between binder and governance documentation.

Workflow

- Lessons learned identified during quarterly binder reviews.
- Improvement actions documented in register with owner role and target date.
- Status tracked as _Planned_, _In Progress_, or _Completed_.
- Register updated quarterly alongside compliance and mitigation registers.

Usage Notes

- Stored in `/docs/investor-presentations/binder-continuous-improvement-register.md`.
- Serves as formal audit evidence of ongoing governance refinement.
- Complements binder compliance register, risk compliance log, and mitigation register.
