# PayMate AI – Investor Governance Audit Evidence Policy

Purpose
Establish formal standards, responsibilities, and retention rules for governance audit evidence.  
Ensures consistency, reliability, and transparency for contributors, board members, and investors.

Scope

This policy applies to all governance audit evidence collected, indexed, summarized, and presented by PayMate AI.  
Evidence includes reports, logs, screenshots, sign‑offs, packets, and dashboards.

Standards

- **Accuracy:** Evidence must be complete, unaltered, and verifiable.
- **Traceability:** Each evidence item must be linked to a control ID and KPI.
- **Consistency:** Evidence stored in standardized formats and directories.
- **Transparency:** Evidence accessible to board members and investors upon request.

Responsibilities

- **Governance Lead:** Oversees evidence collection and compliance with policy.
- **Compliance Officer:** Ensures accessibility and compliance evidence integrity.
- **Investor Relations Lead:** Manages investor packet alignment and transparency evidence.
- **Finance Lead:** Maintains dashboard logs and financial governance evidence.

Retention Rules

- Evidence retained for **3 years** minimum.
- Archived evidence stored securely in `/evidence/archives`.
- Expired evidence reviewed annually and securely disposed of.
- Investor packets retained for **5 years** for historical transparency.

Review & Updates

- Policy reviewed annually during governance audit cycle.
- Updates approved by Governance Lead and board sign‑off.
- Changes communicated to contributors and investors.

Compliance

- Non‑compliance with evidence policy escalated to board.
- Corrective actions documented in action register.
- Policy forms part of governance maturity model and investor assurance framework.
