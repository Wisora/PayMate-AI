# PayMate AI – Investor Presentation Binder Governance Calendar

Purpose
Provide a structured calendar of binder governance activities.  
Ensures quarterly and annual reviews, audits, and sign‑offs are scheduled and tracked consistently.

Governance Calendar Overview

| Period | Activity | Description | Owner Role | Deliverable |

|--------|----------|-------------|------------|-------------|

| Q1 YYYY | Compliance Review | Validate accessibility, certifications, audit trail | Compliance Officer | Compliance Register |
| Q1 YYYY | Risk Log Update | Identify and record risks | Governance Lead | Risk Compliance Log |
| Q2 YYYY | Mitigation Planning | Document planned mitigation strategies | Finance Lead | Mitigation Register |
| Q2 YYYY | Audit Review | Conduct binder audit and log findings | Investor Relations | Audit Register |
| Q3 YYYY | Continuous Improvement Review | Capture lessons learned and improvements | Governance Lead | Continuous Improvement Register |
| Q3 YYYY | Mid‑Cycle Governance Sync | Align binder with governance records | Finance Lead | Governance Sync Notes |
| Q4 YYYY | Annual Review | Consolidate evolution history, lessons, improvements | Governance Lead | Annual Review Report |
| Q4 YYYY | Audit Closing Report | Formal wrap‑up of audit cycle | Compliance Officer | Audit Closing Report |

Annual Activities

- **Annual Review:** Consolidates evolution history, lessons learned, and improvements.
- **Audit Closing Report:** Summarizes corrective actions, variances, and sign‑offs.
- **Governance Master Index Update:** Ensures navigation consistency across all documents.

Workflow

- Calendar maintained by Governance Lead.
- Activities scheduled at start of fiscal year.
- Deliverables stored in `/docs/investor-presentations/`.
- Calendar updated annually alongside Governance Master Index.

Usage Notes

- Stored in `/docs/investor-presentations/binder-governance-calendar.md`.
- Serves as **timeline evidence** for investors and auditors.
- Complements governance master index, annual review, and audit suite.
