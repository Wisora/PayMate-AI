# PayMate AI – Investor Governance Audit Evidence Certification

Purpose
Define certification framework for contributors completing governance evidence training.  
Establishes levels, criteria, and renewal rules to ensure accountability and investor confidence.

Certification Levels

Level 1 – Contributor

- Completed onboarding guide and training module.
- Demonstrated ability to submit evidence using template.
- Passed quiz on glossary and categories.
- Role‑play exercise: Evidence submission and verification.

Level 2 – Evidence Owner

- Maintains responsibility for specific evidence categories.
- Demonstrated ability to verify and sign off evidence.
- Passed assessment on storage and retention rules.
- Role‑play exercise: Board presentation of evidence lifecycle.

Level 3 – Governance Lead

- Oversees evidence policy, procedures, and workflow.
- Demonstrated ability to manage dashboards and investor packets.
- Passed assessment on metrics and data sources.
- Role‑play exercise: Investor Q&A session on maturity model.

Certification Criteria

- Completion of training module exercises and quizzes.
- Successful role‑play scenarios with Governance Lead or Compliance Officer.
- Evidence submission accuracy rate ≥ 95%.
- Verification compliance rate ≥ 90%.

Renewal Rules

- Certification valid for **12 months**.
- Annual refresher training required.
- Renewal assessment includes updated metrics and roadmap.
- Non‑renewal results in contributor reverting to probationary status.

Audit & Accountability

- Certification records logged in evidence register.
- Governance Lead responsible for certification tracking.
- Investor Relations Lead may request certification evidence during audits.
