# PayMate AI – Investor Presentation Binder Approval Register

Purpose
Maintain a formal record of final sign‑offs for the investor presentation binder and diagram.  
Ensures accountability, transparency, and audit evidence of binder validation separate from deck approvals.

Approval Register Format

| Approval ID | Quarter | Date | Binder Version | Governance Lead | Compliance Officer | Investor Relations Lead | Finance Lead | Status |

|-------------|---------|------------|----------------|-----------------|--------------------|-------------------------|--------------|--------|

| BA‑001 | Q1 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | v1.0 | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | Approved |
| BA‑002 | Q2 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | v1.1 | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | Approved |
| BA‑003 | Q3 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | v1.2 | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | Approved |
| BA‑004 | Q4 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | v1.3 | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | ✅ Signed | Approved |

Approval Workflow

1. **Binder Prepared** → Contributors update binder and diagram with latest quarter’s docs.
2. **Review Log Completed** → Comments and revisions tracked in binder review log.
3. **Final Sign‑Off** → Each lead signs approval register entry.
4. **Binder Delivered** → Approved binder included in investor packet.

Usage Notes

- Each quarterly binder must have a unique Approval ID.
- Status updated only when all leads have signed.
- Register stored in `/docs/investor-presentations/binder-approval-register.md`.
- Serves as formal audit evidence of binder accountability.
