# PayMate AI – Investor Presentation Binder Governance Charter

Purpose
Establish the Investor Presentation Binder as a formal governance instrument.  
Defines its role in accountability, transparency, and investor assurance.

Charter Principles

1. **Transparency** → All investor communications must be backed by documented evidence.
2. **Accountability** → Governance, Compliance, Investor Relations, and Finance leads must validate and sign off each binder cycle.
3. **Auditability** → Every binder update, review, and approval must be logged for traceability.
4. **Accessibility** → All binder materials must meet accessibility standards for inclusive investor communication.
5. **Consistency** → Binder maintained quarterly with standardized workflows, visuals, and governance records.

Scope

- Covers all presentation suite documents (deck, style guide, template, checklist, review log, approval register).
- Includes governance evidence chain (metrics, risk register, certifications, audit logs).
- Encompasses governance cycle visuals (workflow diagram, role matrix, calendar, cadence diagram).
- Extends to binder governance records (checklist, review log, approval register, master index, audit trail).

Governance Roles

- **Governance Lead:** Oversees binder completeness and validates evidence chain.
- **Compliance Officer:** Ensures accessibility, compliance, and certification records.
- **Investor Relations Lead:** Aligns binder with investor packet cadence and communication standards.
- **Finance Lead:** Confirms financial accuracy and quarterly sign‑offs.
- **Contributors:** Maintain supporting docs and update binder links.

Governance Cycle

1. **Preparation** → Contributors update presentation suite and governance evidence.
2. **Review** → Governance and compliance reviews logged in binder review log.
3. **Approval** → Formal sign‑offs recorded in binder approval register.
4. **Delivery** → Binder finalized, distributed to investors, and archived for compliance.

Enforcement

- Binder governance charter stored in `/docs/investor-presentations/binder-governance-charter.md`.
- Reviewed annually to ensure alignment with evolving investor and compliance requirements.
- Serves as formal audit evidence of PayMate AI’s governance framework.
