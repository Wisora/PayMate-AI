# PayMate AI – Investor Meeting Agenda

Purpose
A structured agenda for investor meetings.  
Keeps discussions focused, efficient, and aligned with PayMate AI’s vision and roadmap.

1. Welcome & Introduction (5 minutes)

- Brief thank‑you and context
- Vision: multilingual, mobile‑friendly SaaS finance assistant
- Problem: subscription economy challenges + accessibility gap
- Solution: subscription tracking, fraud alerts, multilingual notifications, mobile‑responsive UI

1. Product Demo (10 minutes)

- Login & dashboard overview
- Subscription tracking workflow
- Fraud alert simulation
- Multilingual interface switch
- Contributor‑friendly repo showcase

1. Roadmap & Scaling (5 minutes)

- MVP → Beta → Production → Enterprise timeline
- Scaling strategy: free hosting → paid tiers → Kubernetes
- Compliance checkpoints at each phase

1. Compliance & Trust (5 minutes)

- Current alignment: GDPR, CCPA, POPIA
- Roadmap: SOC 2, ISO 27001
- Security practices: encryption, audit logs, RBAC

1. Market & Business Model (5 minutes)

- Subscription economy growth
- Freemium SaaS → Paid tiers → B2B fintech integrations
- Revenue streams: consumer + enterprise

1. Q&A Session (10 minutes)

- Use `docs/investor-talking-points.md` for quick answers
- Reference `docs/investor-qa.md` for detailed responses
- Share `docs/investor-faq.md` for quick reference

1. Closing Pitch (5 minutes)

- Reinforce vision and traction
- Highlight compliance roadmap
- State funding ask and partnership opportunities
- Deliver closing script (`docs/investor-closing-script.md`)

1. Follow‑Up

- Send thank‑you email (`docs/investor-email-template.md`)
- Share handout + checklist
- Offer deeper dive or demo replay
