# PayMate AI – Investor Presentation Binder Audit Metrics Register

Purpose
Track binder audit metrics across quarters, including targets, thresholds, and variances.  
Provides auditors and governance leads with a detailed performance record for compliance and assurance.

Audit Metrics Register Format

| Metric ID | Quarter | Date | Metric Category | Target | Threshold | Actual | Variance | Reviewer Role | Status |

|-----------|---------|------------|-----------------------|--------|-----------|--------|----------|----------------------|--------|

| AMR‑001 | Q1 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Accessibility Checks | 100% | ≥ 95% | 98% | -2% | Compliance Officer | Passed |
| AMR‑002 | Q2 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Certification Renewals| 100% | ≥ 90% | 100% | +0% | Governance Lead | Passed |
| AMR‑003 | Q3 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Audit Trail Completeness | 100% | ≥ 85% | 90% | -10% | Investor Relations | Improving |
| AMR‑004 | Q4 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Governance Alignment | 100% | ≥ 95% | 100% | +0% | Finance Lead | Passed |

Metric Categories

1. **Accessibility Checks** → Percentage of visuals/documents meeting accessibility standards.
2. **Certification Renewals** → Percentage of certifications current and logged.
3. **Audit Trail Completeness** → Percentage of binder lifecycle actions logged with timestamps.
4. **Governance Alignment** → Percentage of binder records aligned with governance documentation.

Workflow

- Metrics defined at start of fiscal year.
- Targets and thresholds established by governance leads.
- Actuals measured quarterly during binder audit reviews.
- Variances analyzed and corrective actions documented in audit register.

Usage Notes

- Register updated quarterly alongside audit dashboard and audit register.
- Stored in `/docs/investor-presentations/binder-audit-metrics-register.md`.
- Serves as **quantitative audit evidence** for investors and auditors.
