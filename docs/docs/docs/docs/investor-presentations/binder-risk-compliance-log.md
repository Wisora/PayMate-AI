# PayMate AI – Investor Presentation Binder Risk Compliance Log

Purpose
Record risks identified during binder governance cycles, track mitigation actions, and provide assurance of proactive risk management.  
Serves as evidence for auditors, investors, and regulators.

Risk Categories

1. **Accessibility Risks** → Gaps in compliance with accessibility standards.
2. **Certification Risks** → Delays or failures in certification renewals.
3. **Audit Risks** → Incomplete or inconsistent audit trail records.
4. **Governance Risks** → Misalignment between binder and governance records.

Workflow

- Risks identified during quarterly reviews and audits.
- Each risk logged with severity and likelihood ratings.
- Linked mitigation actions tracked in **Mitigation Register**.
- Risk compliance log updated quarterly and referenced in **Governance Calendar**.
