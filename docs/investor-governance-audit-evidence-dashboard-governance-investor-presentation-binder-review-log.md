# PayMate AI – Investor Presentation Binder Review Log

Purpose
Maintain a detailed record of binder updates, reviewer comments, and quarterly confirmations.  
Provides transparency, accountability, and audit evidence of binder maintenance.

Review Log Format

| Review ID | Date | Reviewer Role | Section Reviewed | Comments / Feedback | Status | Linked Docs |

|-----------|------------|----------------------|------------------|---------------------|--------|-------------|

| BR‑001 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Governance Lead | Binder Contents | Verified all links functional | Completed | Master Packet |
| BR‑002 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Compliance Officer | Accessibility | Updated alt text for visuals | Completed | Style Guide |
| BR‑003 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Investor Relations | Evidence Chain | Added latest feedback log | In Progress | Feedback Log |
| BR‑004 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Finance Lead | Approval Register| Confirmed quarterly sign‑off | Completed | Approval Register |
| BR‑005 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Governance Lead | Binder Diagram | Updated roadmap link | Completed | Binder Diagram |

Review Cycle Steps

1. **Binder Update** → Contributors refresh binder and diagram with latest quarter’s docs.
2. **Governance Review** → Governance Lead validates completeness and accuracy.
3. **Compliance Review** → Accessibility and compliance verified.
4. **Investor Relations Review** → Alignment with investor packet confirmed.
5. **Finance Review** → Financial commentary and approvals checked.
6. **Final Confirmation** → All leads sign off binder review log entry.

Usage Notes

- Each review logged with unique ID, date, role, and section.
- Comments tracked for continuous improvement.
- Status updated as _Draft_, _In Progress_, _Completed_.
- Serves as audit evidence of binder maintenance and investor readiness.
