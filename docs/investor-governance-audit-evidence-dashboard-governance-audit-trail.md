# PayMate AI – Governance Dashboard Audit Trail

Purpose
Maintain a detailed log of governance actions across contributors, governance lead, compliance officer, and investor relations.  
Provides transparency, accountability, and audit evidence for external reviewers and investors.

Audit Trail Format

| Action ID | Date | Role | Action | Linked Doc | Reason / Context | Status |

|-----------|------------|-------------------|--------|------------|------------------|--------|

| A‑001 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Contributor | Submitted evidence item | Audit Evidence Log | Quarterly submission deadline | Completed |
| A‑002 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Governance Lead | Validated KPI metrics | Risk Register | Quarterly KPI accuracy check | Completed |
| A‑003 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Compliance Officer| Verified accessibility compliance | Accessibility Checklist | WCAG 2.1 AA requirement | Completed |
| A‑004 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Investor Relations| Prepared investor packet | Investor Packet Outline | Quarterly investor meeting | In Progress |
| A‑005 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Governance Lead | Approved release checklist | Release Checklist | Pre‑release sign‑off | Completed |
| A‑006 | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Investor Relations| Logged investor feedback | Feedback Log | Post‑meeting Q&A | Completed |

Action Categories

- **Evidence Submission:** Contributors submit audit evidence.
- **Validation:** Governance Lead confirms KPI accuracy.
- **Compliance Verification:** Compliance Officer checks accessibility/security.
- **Investor Engagement:** Investor Relations prepares packet, logs feedback.
- **Release Approval:** Governance Lead signs off release checklist.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Lessons learned logged after each cycle.

Usage Notes

- Each action logged with unique ID, date, role, and linked doc.
- Status tracked as _Draft_, _In Progress_, _Completed_.
- Reviewed quarterly during governance meetings.
- Serves as formal audit evidence for external reviewers and investors.
