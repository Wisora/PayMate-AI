# PayMate AI Data Retention Policy

## Overview

This document outlines how long PayMate AI retains various types of user and system data.

## Retention Schedule

| Data Category | Retention Period | Reason |
| ------------- | ---------------- | ------ |

| **User Account Info** | 30 days after closure | Account recovery / Fraud prevention |
| **Transaction Records** | 7 years | Tax and legal compliance |
| **Payment Schedules** | Until deleted by user | Operational necessity |
| **Audit Logs** | 1 year | Security monitoring |
| **AI Chat History** | 90 days | Service improvement |
| **Notification Logs** | 30 days | Troubleshooting |

## Data Deletion Process

When the retention period expires, data is:

1. **Anonymized**: Aggregated for statistical purposes without PII.
2. **Purged**: Permanently removed from production databases and backups.

## User Rights

Users can request a full export of their data or immediate deletion of non-essential records via the Settings panel.
