# PayMate AI – Investor Governance Audit Risk Matrix

Purpose
Provide a visual framework for categorizing governance risks.  
Helps prioritize mitigation by mapping risks across likelihood × impact.

Risk Matrix Grid

| Likelihood ↓ / Impact → | Low Impact | Medium Impact | High Impact |

|--------------------------|------------|---------------|-------------|

| **Low Likelihood** | Monitor | Monitor | Escalate |
| **Medium Likelihood** | Monitor | Actively Manage | Escalate |
| **High Likelihood** | Actively Manage | Escalate | Critical Priority |

Usage Notes

- Matrix updated quarterly alongside `/docs/investor-governance-audit-risk-log.md`.
- Risks plotted to prioritize board and contributor focus.
- Critical Priority risks require immediate mitigation and board oversight.
- Escalate risks flagged for investor transparency in packets.
