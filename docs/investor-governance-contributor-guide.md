# PayMate AI – Investor Governance Contributor Guide

Purpose
Define role‑specific responsibilities for governance updates.  
Ensures Governance Lead, Compliance Officer, Investor Relations, and Finance each know their duties per cycle.

Governance Lead

- Owns governance dashboard updates (`/docs/investor-governance-dashboard.md`)
- Coordinates quarterly and annual verification cycles
- Signs off on governance checklists and dashboards
- Oversees archive maintenance (`/docs/investor-governance-dashboard-history.md`, `/docs/investor-governance-visual-archive-index.md`)

Compliance Officer

- Maintains compliance documentation (`/docs/compliance.md`)
- Verifies ISO 27001, GDPR, SOC 2 status each quarter
- Logs remediation tasks in `/docs/quarterly-verification.md`
- Ensures accessibility compliance across visuals and decks
- Signs off on compliance sections in governance dashboards

Investor Relations Lead

- Updates investor‑facing governance highlights in `/docs/investor-highlights.md`
- Ensures governance outcomes are reflected in investor packets and presentations
- Aligns governance visuals with investor style guide (`/docs/investor-presentation-style-guide.md`)
- Signs off on investor transparency sections in governance dashboards

Finance Lead

- Reviews financial compliance and audit outcomes
- Confirms governance updates align with investor financial reporting
- Signs off on finance sections in governance dashboards and history logs
- Ensures financial governance outcomes are archived properly

Contributor Workflow

1. **Check Governance Update Checklist** → `/docs/investor-governance-update-checklist.md`
2. **Update Dashboard** → `/docs/investor-governance-dashboard.md`
3. **Log Outcomes** → `/docs/investor-governance-history.md` and dashboard history
4. **Generate Visuals** → per `/docs/investor-governance-style-guide.md`
5. **Archive Properly** → update `/docs/investor-governance-visual-archive-index.md`
6. **Sync Master Files** → `/docs/investor-governance-master.md` and `/docs/investor-governance-master-index.md`
7. **Collect Sign‑Offs** → Governance, Compliance, Investor Relations, Finance

Usage Notes

- Each role must complete their section **quarterly**.
- Annual cycle requires deeper audit review and consolidated sign‑off.
- Contributors should cross‑reference investor dashboards to ensure sync.
