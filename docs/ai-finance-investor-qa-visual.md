# PayMate AI – Investor Q&A Visual

Purpose
Provide a scaffold for designing the investor Q&A as a visual FAQ sheet.  
Ensures common investor questions are answered clearly with icons and highlights.

**Header – Investor FAQ Overview**

- ❓ Title: “Investor Q&A”
- 📄 Short intro: “Answers to the most common investor questions about PayMate AI.”

**Section 1 – Performance & KPIs**

- 📊 _How accurate are forecasts?_ → < 5% variance

**Section 2 – Roadmap & Growth**

- 📜 _Are audits performed?_ → SOC 2 quarterly audits

**Section 4 – Accessibility & Inclusivity**

- 🌍 _Is the platform multilingual?_ → English, Afrikaans, French, Spanish
- 🎨 _Accessibility compliance?_ → WCAG AA standards

**Section 5 – Investor Materials**

- 📄 Handout → Quick overview
- 🗂 One‑Pager → Condensed snapshot
- 📦 Package → Full quarterly packet
- 📊 Dashboard → KPI trends
- ❓ Q&A → This sheet

Diagram Elements

- **Icons** → Represent categories (KPIs, roadmap, compliance, accessibility, materials).
- **Color Coding** →
  - Blue → KPIs
  - Orange → Roadmap
  - Green → Compliance
  - Purple → Accessibility
  - Grey → Investor Materials
