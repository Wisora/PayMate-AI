# PayMate AI – Investor Governance Audit Evidence Maturity Model

Purpose
Define the staged progression of governance audit evidence practices.  
Provides investors with a clear framework for evaluating maturity and future trajectory.

Stages of Evidence Maturity

Stage 1 – Initial

- Evidence collected ad hoc, inconsistent formats.
- Limited linkage to KPIs or controls.
- Minimal investor visibility.

Stage 2 – Developing

- Evidence register established with structured categories.
- Quarterly updates begin.
- Evidence linked to controls and KPIs for traceability.
- Investor packets include curated highlights.

Stage 3 – Mature

- Evidence index and summary standardized.
- Dashboard integration with KPI tiles and trend charts.
- Multilingual accessibility evidence included.
- Predictive analytics introduced for evidence trends.
- Investor confidence strengthened through transparency.

Stage 4 – Optimized

- Full automation of evidence collection across all governance dimensions.
- AI‑assisted validation detects anomalies and gaps.
- Interactive dashboards with drill‑down to raw evidence.
- Continuous improvement cycle embedded in governance workflows.
- Evidence practices benchmarked against industry leaders.

Current Position (2026)

- PayMate AI is at **Stage 3 – Mature**, with dashboards, summaries, and predictive analytics in progress.
- Roadmap targets **Stage 4 – Optimized** within 8–12 quarters.

Usage Notes

- Maturity model updated annually alongside governance roadmap.
- Used in board meetings and investor Q&A to demonstrate progress.
- Serves as a benchmark for continuous improvement and industry alignment.
