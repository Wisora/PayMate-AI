# PayMate AI – Governance Dashboard Investor Packet Outline

Purpose
Provide a standardized structure for the quarterly investor packet.  
Ensures all governance dashboard materials are consolidated, polished, and investor‑ready.

Packet Sections

1. Executive Summary

- Quarterly highlights and KPI snapshot.
- Risks and mitigation overview.
- Continuous improvement notes.
- Template: `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-dashboard-investor-summary-template.md`.

1. Release Notes

- Version, release date, and scope.
- Key changes, fixes, and updates.
- Known issues and corrective actions.
- Template: `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-dashboard-release-notes-template.md`.

1. Roadmap & Maturity Model

- Roadmap progression (planned enhancements).
- Current maturity stage and next milestones.
- Templates:
  - `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-dashboard-roadmap.md`
  - `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-dashboard-maturity-model.md`

1. Governance & Operations

- Roles and responsibilities.
- Contributor workflows and playbook.
- Training and certification notes.
- Templates:
  - `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-dashboard-governance-guide.md`
  - `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-dashboard-operations-playbook.md`
  - `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-dashboard-training-module.md`
  - `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-dashboard-contributor-handbook.md`

1. Risk & Improvement Logs

- Risk register entries with mitigation actions.
- Continuous improvement log with lessons learned.
- Templates:
  - `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-dashboard-risk-register.md`
  - `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-dashboard-continuous-improvement-log.md`

1. Investor Engagement

- Feedback log with actions taken.
- Q&A module with responses and status.
- Communication guide and meeting template.
- Templates:
  - `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-dashboard-investor-feedback-log.md`
  - `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-dashboard-investor-QA-module.md`
  - `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-dashboard-investor-communication-guide.md`
  - `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-dashboard-investor-meeting-template.md`

Usage Notes

- Packet compiled quarterly before investor meeting.
- Stored in `/docs/investor-packets/`.
- Serves as audit evidence and investor assurance.
