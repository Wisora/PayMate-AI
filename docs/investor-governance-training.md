# PayMate AI – Investor Governance Training Module

Purpose
Provide onboarding training for new contributors to the governance suite.  
Ensures team members understand compliance, dashboards, checklists, and archives before making live updates.

Training Objectives

- Learn the structure of governance documentation
- Practice updating dashboards and checklists
- Understand compliance and accessibility requirements
- Gain confidence in archiving and verification workflows

Module 1 – Orientation

- Read `/docs/investor-governance-readme.md` for overview
- Explore `/docs/investor-governance-master-index.md` to locate files
- Review `/docs/investor-governance-contributor-guide.md` for role responsibilities

**Exercise:**  
Locate the current governance dashboard and identify the last update date.

Module 2 – Compliance Basics

- Study `/docs/compliance.md` for ISO, GDPR, SOC 2 policies
- Review `/docs/scaling.md` for governance in scaling/migration
- Understand accessibility standards in `/docs/investor-governance-style-guide.md`

**Exercise:**  
Write a mock compliance update for Q2 (no live changes).

Module 3 – Dashboards

- Review `/docs/investor-governance-dashboard.md` (current status)
- Explore `/docs/investor-governance-dashboard-history.md` (past logs)
- Study `/docs/investor-governance-dashboard-master.md` (unified hub)

**Exercise:**  
Draft a sample dashboard entry for a fictional audit outcome.

Module 4 – Visuals & Archives

- Learn design standards in `/docs/investor-governance-style-guide.md`
- Explore archived visuals in `/docs/investor-governance-visual-archive-index.md`
- Practice naming conventions for new visuals

**Exercise:**  
Create a placeholder filename for a governance infographic:  
`governance-report-2026-Q2.png`

Module 5 – Checklists & Verification

- Review `/docs/investor-governance-checklist.md` (compliance tasks)
- Practice `/docs/investor-governance-update-checklist.md` (quarterly routine)
- Study `/docs/quarterly-verification.md` and `/docs/annual-verification.md`

**Exercise:**  
Simulate completing one cycle of the update checklist.

Module 6 – History & Lessons

- Explore `/docs/investor-governance-history.md` (quarterly outcomes)
- Review `/docs/ai-finance-lessons-learned.md` (successes/challenges)
- Study `/docs/ai-finance-final-review.md` (consolidated review)

**Exercise:**  
Write 3 bullet points summarizing a fictional governance cycle.

Module 7 – Sign‑Off Practice

- Understand sign‑off requirements across roles
- Practice filling in sign‑off sections in dashboards and checklists

**Exercise:**  
Assign mock sign‑offs for Governance Lead, Compliance Officer, Investor Relations, Finance.

Completion

- Trainee must complete all exercises before contributing live updates
- Governance Lead reviews training outcomes
- Contributor gains approval to work on live governance suite
