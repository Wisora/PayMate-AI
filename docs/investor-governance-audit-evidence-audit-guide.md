# PayMate AI – Investor Governance Audit Evidence Audit Guide

Purpose
Provide external auditors with a structured guide to review governance audit evidence.  
Ensures transparency, traceability, and consistency across audits.

Audit Scope

- Governance evidence lifecycle (collection → verification → storage → reporting → transparency).
- Evidence categories: Compliance, Accessibility, Security, Verification, Transparency.
- Metrics and KPIs defined in `/docs/investor-governance-audit-evidence-metrics.md`.
- Certification framework for contributors and governance leads.

Audit Preparation

1. Review **Evidence Policy** and **Procedures**.
2. Familiarize with **Handbook** and **Glossary**.
3. Confirm directory structure in `/evidence/`.
4. Identify sample evidence items across categories.

Audit Steps

Step 1 – Evidence Collection

- Verify completeness of logs, reports, screenshots, and sign‑offs.
- Confirm Evidence IDs, Control IDs, and KPI linkage.
- Check adherence to template and checklist.

Step 2 – Evidence Verification

- Review sign‑off records by Governance Lead and Compliance Officer.
- Confirm anomalies logged in risk register.
- Assess verification completion rate.

Step 3 – Evidence Storage

- Inspect directory hierarchy and access controls.
- Confirm retention rules (3 years minimum, 5 years for investor packets).
- Review archive index audits.

Step 4 – Evidence Reporting

- Cross‑check register, index, and summary digest.
- Validate dashboard logs and KPI accuracy.
- Confirm investor packet alignment.

Step 5 – Certification Records

- Review contributor certification levels (Contributor, Owner, Lead).
- Confirm training completion and renewal compliance.
- Validate certification records logged in evidence register.

Audit Deliverables

- **Audit Report:** Findings, compliance status, and recommendations.
- **Action Register:** Corrective actions logged for non‑compliance.
- **Board Packet:** Summary of audit outcomes for investor transparency.

Continuous Improvement

- Benchmark against governance maturity model.
- Recommend automation and AI validation enhancements.
- Align with roadmap for governance evolution.
