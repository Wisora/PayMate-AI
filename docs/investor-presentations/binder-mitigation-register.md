# PayMate AI – Investor Presentation Binder Mitigation Register

Purpose
Document planned mitigation strategies for risks identified in the Risk Compliance Log.  
Provides proactive evidence of governance resilience and investor assurance.

Mitigation Categories

1. **Accessibility Mitigation** → Remediation of accessibility gaps in investor materials.
2. **Certification Mitigation** → Automation and scheduling of certification renewals.
3. **Audit Mitigation** → Automation and monitoring of audit trail completeness.
4. **Governance Mitigation** → Syncing binder records with governance documentation.

Workflow

- Risks logged in **Risk Compliance Log** are linked here with mitigation strategies.
- Each mitigation tracked until completion, with quarterly updates.
- Variances documented in **Audit Variance Log** if mitigation targets are missed.
- Register updated quarterly and referenced in **Governance Calendar**.
