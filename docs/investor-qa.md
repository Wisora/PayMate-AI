# PayMate AI Investor Q&A

Overview
This document prepares answers to common investor questions about PayMate AI.  
It aligns with our roadmap, compliance, scaling, and licensing strategies.

Product

Q: What problem does PayMate AI solve?
A: Consumers struggle to track subscriptions, manage recurring payments, and detect fraud. PayMate AI provides a multilingual, mobile‑friendly finance assistant that addresses these gaps.

Q: How is PayMate AI different from existing finance apps?
A: Unlike traditional apps, PayMate AI is multilingual, accessible, and contributor‑friendly. It combines subscription tracking, fraud alerts, and compliance documentation in one SaaS platform.

Technology

Q: Is this just a demo or a real product?
A: The current repo is an MVP with demo routes and performance tests. The roadmap outlines clear milestones toward production and enterprise scale, including JWT auth, Stripe integration, and Kubernetes orchestration.

Q: How scalable is the architecture?  
A: The scaling guide shows a migration path from free hosting → paid tiers → enterprise Kubernetes. This ensures seamless growth without disrupting contributors or users.

Compliance

Q: How do you handle data privacy?  
A: PayMate AI aligns with GDPR, CCPA, and POPIA. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, audit logs are immutable, and compliance checks are documented in `docs/compliance.md`.

Q: Will you pursue certifications?  
A: Yes, SOC 2 and ISO 27001 compliance are part of the enterprise roadmap (Q1 2027).

Business Model

Q: How will you monetize?  
A: Freemium SaaS model. Free tier for basic subscription tracking, paid tiers for advanced fraud detection and compliance features. B2B integrations with fintechs provide additional revenue streams.

Q: What’s the market opportunity?  
A: The subscription economy is growing rapidly, and personal finance assistants are in demand. PayMate AI fills the multilingual accessibility gap.

Team & Contributors

Q: Who is behind PayMate AI?  
A: Craig, a visionary SaaS founder, supported by open‑source contributors. The repo is structured to be investor‑ready and contributor‑friendly.

Q: How do you attract contributors?  
A: Clear onboarding (`docs/onboarding.md`), contributor guidelines (`CONTRIBUTING.md`), and professional repo polish make it easy for developers to join and add value.

Licensing

Q: What license are you using?  
A: MIT License (recommended for adoption). Apache 2.0 is documented as an alternative for enterprise investors seeking patent protection.

Roadmap

Q: What’s the timeline?
A:

- Q2 2026 → MVP complete
- Q3 2026 → Beta rollout
- Q4 2026 → Production launch
- Q1 2027 → Enterprise scaling

Q: How do you ensure milestones are met?  
A: Milestones are tracked in `docs/roadmap.md` and GitHub Projects. Updates are provided quarterly.

Funding

Q: What are you asking for?  
A: Funding to scale infrastructure, conduct compliance audits, and grow contributor base. Partnerships with fintechs are also sought to accelerate adoption.

Q: How will funds be used?
A: Infrastructure upgrades, compliance certifications, contributor incentives, and investor‑ready polish.
