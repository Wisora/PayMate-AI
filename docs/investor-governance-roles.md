# PayMate AI – Investor Governance Roles

Purpose
Formally define governance roles, responsibilities, authority, and scope.  
Ensures clarity and accountability across compliance, dashboards, and investor reporting.

Governance Lead

Responsibilities

- Owns governance framework and quarterly update cycle
- Oversees dashboards, checklists, and governance master files
- Coordinates sign‑offs across all roles
- Maintains governance archives and visual standards

Authority

- Approves governance dashboards and history logs
- Directs contributors on governance updates
- Final decision‑maker for governance documentation

Scope

- Governance dashboards, checklists, master hub, archives

Compliance Officer

Responsibilities

- Maintains compliance documentation (`/docs/compliance.md`)
- Verifies ISO 27001, GDPR, SOC 2 status each quarter
- Logs remediation tasks in verification files
- Ensures accessibility compliance across visuals and decks

Authority

- Approves compliance sections in dashboards and checklists
- Directs contributors on compliance updates
- Signs off on quarterly and annual verification cycles

Scope

- Compliance docs, verification cycles, accessibility standards

Investor Relations Lead

Responsibilities

- Updates investor‑facing governance highlights
- Ensures governance outcomes are reflected in investor packets
- Aligns governance visuals with investor presentation style guide
- Communicates governance wins to investors

Authority

- Approves investor transparency sections in dashboards
- Directs contributors on investor‑facing governance updates
- Signs off on investor packets and highlights

Scope

- Investor packets, highlights, presentation decks

Finance Lead

Responsibilities

- Reviews financial compliance and audit outcomes
- Confirms governance updates align with investor financial reporting
- Signs off on finance sections in dashboards and history logs
- Ensures financial governance outcomes are archived properly

Authority

- Approves finance sections in governance dashboards
- Directs contributors on financial governance updates
- Signs off on annual governance reviews

Scope

- Finance compliance, audit outcomes, investor financial reporting

Usage Notes

- Roles must collaborate each quarter during governance updates.
- Annual cycle requires consolidated sign‑off across all roles.
- Role definitions should be reviewed annually for relevance.
