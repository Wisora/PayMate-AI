# PayMate AI – Governance ↔ Investor Sync Checklist

Purpose
Provide a consolidated sync checklist.  
Ensures governance and investor cycles are explicitly cross‑linked and aligned across quarterly, annual, and final stages before investor reviews.

Quarterly Sync

- [ ] Governance quarterly logs synced with investor highlights
- [ ] Board oversight notes cross‑referenced with investor Q&A
- [ ] Governance risks flagged + investor mitigation strategies documented
- [ ] Quarterly governance visuals included in investor packets

Annual Sync

- [ ] Governance annual report synced with investor annual report
- [ ] Governance milestones aligned with investor roadmap visuals
- [ ] Governance audit outcomes cross‑referenced with investor compliance updates
- [ ] Annual governance highlights integrated into investor packet

Final Sync Review

- [ ] Governance checklist confirmed in master final review
- [ ] Investor checklist confirmed in master final review
- [ ] Governance visuals cross‑checked against investor packet
- [ ] Infographic summary prepared for joint governance + investor materials
- [ ] Accessibility standards applied (alt text, multilingual captions, screen reader compatibility)

Continuous Sync Loop

- [ ] Quarterly governance logs synced with quarterly investor highlights
- [ ] Annual governance report synced with annual investor report
- [ ] Final sync review outcomes fed into next year’s governance + investor roadmap
- [ ] Lessons learned documented + integrated into contributor workflows
