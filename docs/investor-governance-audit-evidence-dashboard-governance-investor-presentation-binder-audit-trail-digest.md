# PayMate AI – Investor Presentation Binder Audit Trail Digest

Purpose
Provide a condensed summary of binder audit trail entries.  
Designed for auditors and regulators who require a quick reference to binder lifecycle actions.

Digest Format

| Entry ID | Quarter | Date | Action Type | Description | Owner Role | Status |

|----------|---------|------------|----------------------|-------------|-------------------|--------|

| ATD‑001 | Q1 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Accessibility Update | Alt text added to visuals | Compliance Officer | Completed |
| ATD‑002 | Q2 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Certification Renewal | Certification renewed and logged | Governance Lead | Completed |
| ATD‑003 | Q3 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Audit Trail Logging | Automated logging implemented | Investor Relations | In Progress |
| ATD‑004 | Q4 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Governance Sync | Risk register aligned with binder | Finance Lead | Completed |

Action Categories

1. **Accessibility Updates** → Ensuring visuals and documents meet accessibility standards.
2. **Certification Renewals** → Maintaining current certifications in evidence chain.
3. **Audit Trail Logging** → Strengthening completeness and automation of audit logs.
4. **Governance Syncs** → Aligning binder with governance records mid‑cycle.

Workflow

- Digest compiled quarterly from full audit trail.
- Summarizes key binder lifecycle actions for quick auditor reference.
- Complements detailed audit register and audit summary.

Usage Notes

- Stored in `/docs/investor-presentations/binder-audit-trail-digest.md`.
- Serves as **condensed audit evidence** for external auditors and regulators.
- Updated quarterly alongside audit register and compliance register.
