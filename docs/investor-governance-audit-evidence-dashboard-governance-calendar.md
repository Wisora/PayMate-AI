# PayMate AI – Governance Dashboard Governance Calendar

Purpose
Provide a quarterly calendar of governance activities.  
Ensures contributors, auditors, and investors have clear visibility into deadlines and milestones.

Quarterly Cycle Overview

| Week | Activity | Owner | Linked Docs |

|------|----------|-------|-------------|

| Week 1 | Evidence submission deadline | Contributors | Audit Evidence Log |
| Week 2 | KPI validation & anomaly review | Governance Lead | Risk Register |
| Week 3 | Accessibility & performance checks | Compliance Officer | Accessibility Checklist |
| Week 4 | Risk register update | Governance Lead | Risk Register |
| Week 5 | Continuous improvement log update | Contributors | Continuous Improvement Log |
| Week 6 | Certification renewal check | Compliance Officer | Certification Register |
| Week 7 | Investor packet preparation | Investor Relations Lead | Investor Packet Outline |
| Week 8 | Quarterly investor meeting | Investor Relations Lead | Meeting Template, Summary |
| Week 9 | Feedback log update | Investor Relations Lead | Feedback Log, Q&A Module |
| Week 10 | Roadmap & maturity model review | Governance Lead | Roadmap, Maturity Model |

Key Milestones

- **Evidence Submission:** Week 1 of each quarter.
- **Risk Review:** Week 4.
- **Investor Packet Finalization:** Week 7.
- **Investor Meeting:** Week 8.
- **Feedback Integration:** Week 9.
- **Roadmap Review:** Week 10.

Usage Notes

- Calendar repeated each quarter (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4).
- Dates adjusted based on fiscal year and investor meeting schedule.
- Serves as audit evidence of structured governance cadence.
