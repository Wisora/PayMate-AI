# PayMate AI – Investor Q&A (Finance)

Purpose
Anticipate and answer common investor questions about PayMate AI’s finance automation.  
Ensures transparency, consistency, and confidence in investor communications.

General Questions
**Q: How does PayMate AI handle financial data securely?**  
A: All data is encrypted with AES‑256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Keys are rotated quarterly and stored in secure vaults. Audit trails are immutable and reviewed quarterly.

**Q: What makes PayMate AI’s finance workflows investor‑ready?**  
A: Each workflow is documented, QA‑checked, and aligned with compliance standards. Governance approvals ensure human oversight before automation is released.

Compliance & Security
**Q: Which compliance frameworks does PayMate AI follow?**  
A: PCI‑DSS for payment data, GDPR/POPIA/CCPA for privacy, SOC 2 and ISO 27001 for enterprise security. Certifications are tracked in `docs/ai-finance-compliance.md`.

**Q: How are incidents handled?**  
A: Suspected breaches trigger a 24‑hour SLA response. Incidents are logged in `docs/ai-finance-security-log.md` and remediation steps are shared with clients + investors.

Roadmap & Evolution
**Q: What’s next for PayMate AI’s finance automation?**  
A: Predictive analytics and investor dashboards in 2026, portfolio optimization in 2027, and interactive investor portals beyond 2027. See `docs/ai-finance-roadmap.md`.

**Q: How often are workflows updated?**  
A: Quarterly, following the `docs/ai-finance-update-checklist.md`. Updates include retraining models, refreshing dashboards, and syncing compliance logs.

Quality & Accessibility
**Q: How do you ensure AI outputs are accurate and unbiased?**  
A: Outputs are validated against source data, bias monitoring scripts are run, and QA audits are performed quarterly. See `docs/ai-finance-quality.md`.

**Q: Are dashboards accessible to all investors?**  
A: Yes. Dashboards meet WCAG AA standards, include alt text, multilingual captions, and high‑contrast themes. See `docs/ai-finance-accessibility.md`.

Investor Transparency
**Q: How can investors track progress?**  
A: Through quarterly KPI updates (`docs/ai-finance-metrics.md`), dashboards (`docs/ai-finance-dashboard.md`), and handouts/one‑pagers for quick reference.

**Q: Where can I find a summary of all finance docs?**  
A: In `docs/ai-finance-summary.md` and `docs/ai-finance-index.md`, which provide a central overview and navigation guide.
