# PayMate AI – Investor Presentation Binder Audit Corrective Actions Register

Purpose
Track corrective actions arising from audit findings until closure.  
Provides auditors and governance leads with a structured record of remediation efforts and accountability.

Corrective Actions Register Format

| Action ID | Quarter | Date | Audit Finding | Corrective Action | Owner Role | Target Date | Status | Closure Date |

|-----------|---------|------------|---------------|------------------|-------------------|-------------|--------|--------------|

| CAR‑001 | Q1 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Accessibility gaps in visuals | Introduced accessibility checklist | Compliance Officer | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Completed | YYYY‑MM‑DD |
| CAR‑002 | Q2 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Certification renewal delayed | Automated certification alerts | Governance Lead | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Completed | YYYY‑MM‑DD |
| CAR‑003 | Q3 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Missing audit trail entries | Implemented automated logging | Investor Relations | YYYY‑MM‑DD | In Progress | — |
| CAR‑004 | Q4 YYYY | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Risk register misaligned | Scheduled mid‑cycle governance sync | Finance Lead | YYYY‑MM‑DD | Completed | YYYY‑MM‑DD |

Action Categories

1. **Accessibility Actions** → Address gaps in inclusive communication standards.
2. **Certification Actions** → Resolve lapses in certification renewals.
3. **Audit Trail Actions** → Correct incomplete or missing lifecycle entries.
4. **Governance Actions** → Align binder with governance records and cadence.

Workflow

- Audit findings documented in **Audit Register**.
- Corrective actions assigned to owner roles with target dates.
- Status tracked until closure, with closure date recorded.
- Register updated quarterly alongside audit variance log and audit metrics register.

Usage Notes

- Stored in `/docs/investor-presentations/binder-audit-corrective-actions-register.md`.
- Serves as **remediation evidence** for auditors and investors.
- Complements audit register, variance log, and audit dashboard.
