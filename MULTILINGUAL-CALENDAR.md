# Multilingual Calendar – PayMate AI

Purpose
Provide a structured calendar of localization, translation, and multilingual compliance cycles.  
Ensures predictable cadence for contributors, governance leads, and investors.

Quarterly Multilingual Activities

- **Q1 (January – March)**
  - Baseline translation audit (EN/ES/FR).
  - Contributor onboarding guides localized.
  - Investor packet updated with multilingual evidence.

- **Q2 (April – June)**
  - Mid‑year localization compliance review.
  - Documentation translation cycle (new features).
  - Governance binder updated with multilingual cycle evidence.

- **Q3 (July – September)**
  - Roadmap milestone localization review.
  - Accessibility audit across supported languages.
  - Investor Q&A session on multilingual governance.

- **Q4 (October – December)**
  - Annual consolidated multilingual audit.
  - Board deck finalized with multilingual evidence.
  - Lessons learned integrated into CONTINUOUS-IMPROVEMENT.md.

Annual Multilingual Reviews

- Consolidated multilingual evidence presented in board deck.
- Strategic multilingual roadmap updated for next year.
- Continuous improvement cycle documented in EVOLUTION.md.

Ad‑Hoc Multilingual Activities

- **Critical Localization Failures** → Escalated immediately with remediation plan.
- **Security Localization Issues** → Logged in SECURITY-CALENDAR.md.
- **Compliance Localization Gaps** → Cross‑referenced with COMPLIANCE-CALENDAR.md.

Usage

- **Contributors** → Follow quarterly multilingual reviews in workflows.
- **Governance Leads** → Ensure localization evidence logged and escalated.
- **Investors** → Review calendar for confidence in multilingual governance.

Review Cadence

- **Quarterly** → Multilingual calendar updated with audits and evidence.
- **Annual** → Consolidated calendar presented in board deck.
