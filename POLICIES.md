# Policies – PayMate AI

Purpose
Define formal policies for contributors, governance, compliance, and investor relations.  
Provides enforceable standards that ensure professionalism, transparency, and accountability.

Contributor Policies

- **Commit Standards** → All commits must follow commitlint rules.
- **Documentation** → Contributors must update README, STYLE-GUIDE, and DIAGRAMS when workflows change.
- **Onboarding** → New contributors must review ONBOARDING.md before submitting PRs.
- **Code of Conduct** → Respectful, inclusive collaboration enforced across repo and discussions.

Governance Policies

- **Cadence** → Quarterly governance reviews are mandatory; annual consolidation required.
- **Registers** → Risk, audit, compliance, security, change, and decisions registers must remain current.
- **Ownership** → Every action logged with a clear owner in registers.
- **Evidence** → Governance decisions must be backed by audit logs and metrics.

Compliance Policies

- **Accessibility** → WCAG 2.1 AA compliance enforced across all repo assets.
- **Multilingual** → English, Spanish, French minimum; expansion roadmap required.
- **Regulatory** → GDPR and financial regulations adhered to at all times.
- **CI/CD Enforcement** → Compliance checks integrated into pipeline; failures block merges.

Investor Policies

- **Investor‑Ready Repo** → Repo must remain polished with governance suite and registers.
- **Localized Decks** → Investor decks must be available in English, Spanish, French.
- **Transparency** → Metrics, audits, and lessons learned shared openly in quarterly reviews.
- **Q&A Preparation** → Investor relations lead prepares board responses using DECISIONS.md and LESSONS-LEARNED.md.

Enforcement

- **Violations** → Logged in RISK-REGISTER.md and addressed in AUDIT-REGISTER.md.
- **Corrective Actions** → Documented in CHANGE-REGISTER.md.
- **Continuous Monitoring** → CI/CD pipeline enforces compliance and contributor policies.

Review Cadence

- **Quarterly** → Policies reviewed alongside governance binder.
- **Annual** → Consolidated policies presented in board deck.
