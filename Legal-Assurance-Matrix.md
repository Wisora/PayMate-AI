# Legal Assurance Matrix – PayMate AI

Purpose
Benchmark PayMate AI’s compliance, licensing, and legal governance maturity against competitors.  
Track regulatory coverage, licensing models, and investor confidence to ensure PayMate AI leads in legal resilience.

Legal Categories

- **Compliance Coverage** → GDPR, CCPA, POPIA, financial regulations.
- **Licensing Models** → Contributor agreements, open-source licenses, enterprise contracts.
- **Dispute Handling** → Resolution protocols, escalation cycles.
- **Investor Confidence** → Transparency of disclosures and governance binder evidence.
- **Governance Maturity** → Integration of legal protocols into registers and calendars.
