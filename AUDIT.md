# Audit Evidence Register – PayMate AI

Purpose
Provide a consolidated view of audit evidence, variances, and corrective actions.  
Ensures transparency, accountability, and investor confidence in governance processes.

Audit Evidence Suite

- **Audit Dashboard** → High‑level metrics and compliance status.
- **Metrics Register** → Detailed audit metrics and KPIs.
- **Variance Log** → Records deviations from expected governance or compliance.
- **Corrective Actions Register** → Tracks remediation steps and owners.
- **Closing Report** → Summarizes audit cycle outcomes.
- **Master Index** → Navigation scaffold for all audit evidence.

Audit Cycle

1. **Evidence Collection** → Metrics, logs, registers.
2. **Variance Identification** → Deviations flagged in Variance Log.
3. **Corrective Actions** → Assigned and tracked until closure.
4. **Closing Report** → Consolidated summary for board and investors.

Review Cadence

- **Quarterly Audit Reviews** → Variance and corrective action updates.
- **Annual Audit Consolidation** → Closing report integrated into governance binder.
- **Investor Presentation** → Audit evidence summarized in board deck.
