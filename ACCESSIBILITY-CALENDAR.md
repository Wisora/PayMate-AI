# Accessibility Calendar – PayMate AI

Purpose
Provide a structured calendar of accessibility audits, WCAG compliance cycles, and remediation updates.  
Ensures predictable cadence for contributors, governance leads, and investors.

Quarterly Accessibility Activities

- **Q1 (January – March)**
  - Accessibility regression audit (WCAG 2.2 baseline).
  - Contributor training on accessibility standards.
  - Investor packet updated with accessibility evidence.

- **Q2 (April – June)**
  - Mid‑year accessibility compliance review.
  - Documentation accessibility audit (localized guides).
  - Governance binder updated with accessibility cycle evidence.

- **Q3 (July – September)**
  - Accessibility roadmap milestone review.
  - Multilingual accessibility audit (EN/ES/FR).
  - Investor Q&A session on accessibility governance.

- **Q4 (October – December)**
  - Annual consolidated accessibility audit.
  - Board deck finalized with accessibility evidence.
  - Lessons learned integrated into CONTINUOUS-IMPROVEMENT.md.

Annual Accessibility Reviews

- Consolidated accessibility evidence presented in board deck.
- Strategic accessibility roadmap updated for next year.
- Continuous improvement cycle documented in EVOLUTION.md.

Ad‑Hoc Accessibility Activities

- **Critical Accessibility Failures** → Escalated immediately with remediation plan.
- **Security Accessibility Issues** → Logged in SECURITY-CALENDAR.md.
- **Compliance Accessibility Gaps** → Cross‑referenced with COMPLIANCE-CALENDAR.md.

Usage

- **Contributors** → Follow quarterly accessibility reviews in workflows.
- **Governance Leads** → Ensure accessibility evidence logged and escalated.
- **Investors** → Review calendar for confidence in accessibility governance.

Review Cadence

- **Quarterly** → Accessibility calendar updated with audits and evidence.
- **Annual** → Consolidated calendar presented in board deck.
