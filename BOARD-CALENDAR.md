# Board Calendar – PayMate AI

Purpose
Provide a structured calendar of board meetings, investor updates, and governance presentations.  
Ensures predictable cadence for contributors, governance leads, and investors.

Quarterly Board Activities

- **Q1 (January – March)**
  - Board meeting: Governance binder review.
  - Investor packet update with Q1 metrics.
  - Risk register and compliance evidence presented.

- **Q2 (April – June)**
  - Board meeting: Mid‑year strategy alignment.
  - Investor deck localized and shared (EN/ES/FR).
  - CI/CD and accessibility compliance updates.

- **Q3 (July – September)**
  - Board meeting: Roadmap milestone review.
  - Investor Q&A session with updated INVESTOR-QA.md.
  - Security governance and vulnerability disclosures.

- **Q4 (October – December)**
  - Annual board meeting: Consolidated governance binder.
  - Investor packet finalized with fiscal and compliance evidence.
  - Board deck presented with lessons learned and evolution cycle.

Annual Board Review

- Consolidated governance binder presented.
- Annual investor packet delivered.
- Strategic roadmap updated for next year.
- Continuous improvement cycle documented in CONTINUOUS-IMPROVEMENT.md.

Ad‑Hoc Board Activities

- **Critical Risks** → Escalated immediately.
- **Security Vulnerabilities** → Disclosed per SECURITY-POLICY.md timelines.
- **Investor Requests** → Addressed via INVESTOR-QA.md updates.

Usage

- **Contributors** → Prepare evidence for quarterly board reviews.
- **Governance Leads** → Ensure registers and binders updated.
- **Investors** → Review calendar for confidence in oversight maturity.

Review Cadence

- **Quarterly** → Board calendar updated with meeting outcomes.
- **Annual** → Consolidated calendar presented in board deck.
