# Frequently Asked Questions – PayMate AI

Purpose
Provide quick answers to common contributor and investor questions.  
This FAQ complements SUPPORT.md and GOVERNANCE.md by offering clarity at a glance.

Contributors

**Q: How do I start contributing?**  
A: Read **CONTRIBUTING.md** for workflow standards, then open a PR following commitlint rules.

**Q: How are contributors recognized?**  
A: See **CONTRIBUTORS.md** for roles and acknowledgements. Major contributions are noted in **CHANGELOG.md** and **RELEASE.md**.

**Q: What standards must my documentation follow?**  
A: All Markdown must pass **.markdownlint.json** checks. Accessibility standards are outlined in **COMPLIANCE.md**.

**Q: Who reviews my PRs?**  
A: PRs are automatically routed via **CODEOWNERS** to the appropriate reviewer.

Governance & Compliance

**Q: How often are governance reviews conducted?**  
A: Quarterly reviews with annual consolidation. See **GOVERNANCE.md** for cadence.

**Q: How are risks tracked?**  
A: Risks are documented in **RISK.md** with severity levels and mitigation strategies.

**Q: Where can I find audit evidence?**  
A: See **AUDIT.md** for metrics, variance logs, and corrective actions.

**Q: How is compliance ensured?**  
A: Accessibility, multilingual, and regulatory compliance are tracked in **COMPLIANCE.md**.

Security

**Q: How do I report a vulnerability?**  
A: Follow instructions in **SECURITY.md**. Do not open a public issue.

**Q: How quickly will my report be acknowledged?**  
A: Within 48 hours for security disclosures.

Investors

**Q: Where can I see the roadmap?**  
A: **ROADMAP.md** outlines near‑term, mid‑term, and long‑term milestones.

**Q: How are releases communicated?**  
A: Technical history is in **CHANGELOG.md**; investor‑friendly announcements are in **RELEASE.md**.

**Q: Who should I contact for investor queries?**  
A: Email **<investors@paymateai.com>** or see **SUPPORT.md** for escalation paths.
