# Governance Calendar – PayMate AI

Purpose
Provide a structured calendar of governance, compliance, and investor review dates.  
Ensures predictable cadence for contributors, governance leads, and investors.

Quarterly Reviews

- **Q1 (January – March)**
  - Risk, audit, compliance, and security registers updated.
  - Investor packet refreshed with metrics and lessons learned.
  - Governance binder reviewed and consolidated.

- **Q2 (April – June)**
  - Accessibility and multilingual audits completed.
  - CI/CD and quality assurance reports validated.
  - Investor deck localized and shared with board.

- **Q3 (July – September)**
  - Mid‑year strategy alignment with roadmap milestones.
  - Security vulnerability scans and patch cycles reviewed.
  - Lessons learned consolidated into EVOLUTION.md.

- **Q4 (October – December)**
  - Annual consolidation of governance binder.
  - Investor packet finalized for board presentation.
  - Board deck prepared with localized investor materials.

Annual Reviews

- **December Board Review**
  - Consolidated governance binder presented.
  - Annual investor packet delivered.
  - Strategic roadmap updated for next year.
  - Lessons learned and evolution tracked for continuous improvement.

Ad‑Hoc Reviews

- **Critical Risks** → Escalated immediately to board.
- **Security Vulnerabilities** → Disclosed and patched per SECURITY-POLICY.md timelines.
- **Investor Requests** → Q&A updated in INVESTOR-QA.md as needed.
