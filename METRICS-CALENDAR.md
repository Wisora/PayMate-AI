# Metrics Calendar – PayMate AI

Purpose
Provide a structured calendar of KPI reviews, dashboard updates, and investor metric reporting.  
Ensures predictable cadence for contributors, governance leads, and investors.

Quarterly Metrics Reviews

- **Q1 (January – March)**
  - KPI baseline review (subscription growth, accessibility compliance).
  - Metrics dashboard update with Q1 data.
  - Investor packet updated with performance evidence.

- **Q2 (April – June)**
  - Mid‑year KPI trend analysis.
  - CI/CD reliability metrics logged in dashboard.
  - Investor update with growth and compliance metrics.

- **Q3 (July – September)**
  - KPI variance analysis against roadmap milestones.
  - Accessibility and multilingual metrics review.
  - Investor Q&A session on performance governance.

- **Q4 (October – December)**
  - Annual KPI consolidation.
  - Metrics dashboard finalized for board deck.
  - Lessons learned integrated into CONTINUOUS-IMPROVEMENT.md.

Annual Metrics Reviews

- Consolidated KPI evidence presented in board deck.
- Strategic metrics roadmap updated for next year.
- Continuous improvement cycle documented in EVOLUTION.md.

Ad‑Hoc Metrics Activities

- **Critical KPI Drops** → Escalated immediately with investor notification.
- **Security Metrics** → Logged in SECURITY-CALENDAR.md and dashboard.
- **Compliance Metrics** → Cross‑referenced with COMPLIANCE-CALENDAR.md.

Usage

- **Contributors** → Align quarterly work with KPI reviews.
- **Governance Leads** → Ensure metrics logged in METRICS-DASHBOARD.md.
- **Investors** → Review calendar for confidence in performance governance.

Review Cadence

- **Quarterly** → Metrics calendar updated with KPI outcomes.
- **Annual** → Consolidated calendar presented in board deck.
