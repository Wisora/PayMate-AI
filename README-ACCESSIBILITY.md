Accessibility Governance Principles

1. **Standards**
   - Accessibility requirements defined in ACCESSIBILITY-GUIDE.md.
   - Contributors required to follow accessibility practices in workflows.

2. **Evidence Logging**
   - Accessibility audits and remediation actions logged in ACCESSIBILITY-REGISTER.md.
   - Linked to compliance and quality registers for traceability.

3. **Cadence**
   - Quarterly accessibility reviews tracked in ACCESSIBILITY-CALENDAR.md.
   - Annual consolidated accessibility audit presented in board deck.

4. **Transparency**
   - Accessibility evidence consolidated annually in governance binder.
   - Investor disclosures aligned with accessibility cadence.
