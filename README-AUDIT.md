Audit Governance Principles

1. **Independence**
   - Audits conducted independently of contributor workflows.
   - Findings logged in AUDIT-REGISTER.md.

2. **Traceability**
   - Audit evidence linked to compliance, quality, security, and financial domains.
   - Consolidated annually in board deck.

3. **Cadence**
   - Quarterly audit reviews tracked in AUDIT-CALENDAR.md.
   - Annual consolidated audit governance presented to board and investors.

4. **Transparency**
   - Audit evidence consolidated annually in governance binder.
   - Investor disclosures aligned with audit cadence.

Workflow Integration

- Audit findings → logged in register.
- Reviews and cycles → tracked in calendar.
- Linked evidence → compliance, quality, security, and financial registers.
- Consolidated evidence → governance binder and board deck.
