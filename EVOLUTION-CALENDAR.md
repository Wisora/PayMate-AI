# Evolution Calendar – PayMate AI

Purpose
Map the historical progression of PayMate AI across years.  
Highlights major milestones, governance upgrades, and investor‑facing improvements for contributors and investors.

Yearly Milestones

- **2025**
  - Initial repo scaffolding created.
  - Core product vision defined: multilingual, accessible finance assistant.
  - Early governance framework drafted (GOVERNANCE.md, VALUES.md).

- **2026**
  - Governance suite expanded → GOVERNANCE-BINDER.md introduced.
  - Investor suite formalized → INVESTOR-PACKET.md and BOARD-DECK.md created.
  - Risk management strengthened → RISK-REGISTER.md, RISK-MATRIX.md, and RISK-HEATMAP.md added.
  - Accessibility governance → ACCESSIBILITY-GUIDE.md introduced.
  - Continuous improvement cycle → LESSONS-CALENDAR.md and CONTINUOUS-IMPROVEMENT.md established.
  - Metrics transparency → METRICS-DASHBOARD.md consolidated KPIs.

- **2027 (Planned)**
  - Expansion to 10+ languages.
  - Accessibility compliance extended to WCAG 2.2.
  - Governance calendar aligned with investor reporting cycles.
  - Enhanced CI/CD automation with AI‑driven testing.

Governance Evolution

- From ad‑hoc registers → consolidated GOVERNANCE-BINDER.md.
- From informal lessons → structured LESSONS-CALENDAR.md and CONTINUOUS-IMPROVEMENT.md.
- From static metrics → dynamic METRICS-DASHBOARD.md.

Investor Evolution

- From basic roadmap → INVESTOR-PACKET.md bundling all evidence.
- From single deck → BOARD-DECK.md localized in EN/ES/FR.
- From reactive Q&A → INVESTOR-QA.md with prepared responses.

Usage

- **Contributors** → Understand historical context and future direction.
- **Governance Leads** → Track evolution for continuous improvement.
- **Investors** → Review evolution calendar for confidence in long‑term growth.

Review Cadence

- **Annual** → Evolution calendar updated with milestones.
- **Board Deck** → Consolidated evolution presented annually.
