# Communications Calendar – PayMate AI

Purpose
Provide a structured calendar of internal and external communications.  
Ensures predictable cadence for team updates, investor notices, and compliance disclosures.

Quarterly Communications

- **Q1 (January – March)**
  - Internal team update: Accessibility regression fixes.
  - Investor notice: Q1 metrics and compliance evidence.
  - Governance communication: Binder review shared with contributors.

- **Q2 (April – June)**
  - Internal team update: Multilingual onboarding improvements.
  - Investor notice: Mid‑year roadmap alignment.
  - Compliance disclosure: CI/CD pipeline audit results.

- **Q3 (July – September)**
  - Internal team update: Roadmap milestone progress.
  - Investor notice: Security governance and vulnerability disclosures.
  - Governance communication: Contributor feedback survey results.

- **Q4 (October – December)**
  - Internal team update: Annual contributor onboarding refresh.
  - Investor notice: Consolidated fiscal and compliance evidence.
  - Compliance disclosure: Annual GDPR and accessibility audit.

Annual Communications

- Consolidated investor packet delivered.
- Annual board deck presented with governance evidence.
- Strategic roadmap updated for next year.
- Continuous improvement cycle documented in CONTINUOUS-IMPROVEMENT.md.

Ad‑Hoc Communications

- **Critical Risks** → Immediate investor and contributor notification.
- **Security Vulnerabilities** → Disclosure per SECURITY-POLICY.md timelines.
- **Investor Requests** → Addressed via INVESTOR-QA.md updates.

Usage

- **Contributors** → Follow quarterly communications for workflow alignment.
- **Governance Leads** → Ensure communications logged in COMMUNICATIONS.md.
- **Investors** → Review calendar for confidence in governance cadence.

Review Cadence

- **Quarterly** → Communications calendar updated with notices and updates.
- **Annual** → Consolidated calendar presented in board deck.
