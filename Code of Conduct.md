# Code of Conduct

Our Pledge

We pledge to make PayMate AI a welcoming, safe, and professional environment for contributors, users, and investors.  
We are committed to fostering an open and inclusive community, free from harassment or discrimination.

Standards
Contributors are expected to:

- Use respectful and inclusive language.
- Provide constructive feedback and collaborate in good faith.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Focus on clarity, accessibility, and professionalism in code and documentation.
- Avoid personal attacks, trolling, or disruptive behavior.

Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Clarifying standards of acceptable behavior.
- Taking appropriate and fair corrective action when misconduct occurs.
- Ensuring decisions are transparent and documented.

Scope
This Code of Conduct applies:

- Within project repositories, issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- In public spaces where contributors represent the project (conferences, online forums, etc.).

Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.  
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Consequences

- Warning: For minor infractions.
- Temporary Ban: For repeated or serious violations.
- Permanent Ban: For severe misconduct or harassment.

Attribution
This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
