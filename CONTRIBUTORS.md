# Contributors – PayMate AI

Purpose
Acknowledge contributors, define roles, and ensure transparency in responsibilities.  
This register complements GOVERNANCE.md and CODEOWNERS by providing visibility into who drives the project.

Core Team

- **Craig Abels (Founder, Visionary SaaS Lead)**  
  Role: Product design, repo orchestration, governance strategy, investor relations.  
  Responsibilities: Repo structure, onboarding guides, pitch deck preparation, governance binder integrity.

- **Governance Lead**  
  Role: Oversees governance cadence and binder updates.  
  Responsibilities: Quarterly sign‑offs, governance master index, compliance validation.

- **Compliance Officer**  
  Role: Ensures regulatory, accessibility, and security compliance.  
  Responsibilities: WCAG audits, dependency scans, corrective actions register.

- **Investor Relations Lead**  
  Role: Prepares investor decks and manages communications.  
  Responsibilities: Board presentations, multilingual localization, investor Q&A.

- **Finance Lead**  
  Role: Oversees financial governance and risk mitigation.  
  Responsibilities: Subscription tracking accuracy, fraud alert validation, annual review consolidation.

Contributors

- **Community Contributors**  
  Role: Code improvements, documentation updates, accessibility enhancements.  
  Responsibilities: Follow CONTRIBUTING.md, commitlint rules, and markdownlint standards.

- **Design Contributors**  
  Role: Visual assets, diagrams, repo maps, and accessibility‑friendly visuals.  
  Responsibilities: Maintain WCAG compliance, provide multilingual diagrams, align with style guide.

Contribution Standards

- All commits must follow **commitlint.config.js** rules.
- Documentation must pass **.markdownlint.json** checks.
- PRs are routed via **CODEOWNERS** for review.
- Contributors are acknowledged in **RELEASE.md** for each version.

Recognition

- Contributors who responsibly disclose vulnerabilities are credited in **SECURITY.md**.
- Major governance or compliance contributions are noted in **CHANGELOG.md**.
- Visual and design contributions are highlighted in investor decks.
