# Risk Calendar – PayMate AI

Purpose
Provide a structured calendar of risk reviews, escalations, and mitigation updates.  
Ensures predictable cadence for contributors, governance leads, and investors.

Quarterly Risk Reviews

- **Q1 (January – March)**
  - Risk register review and update.
  - Accessibility regression risk assessment.
  - Investor packet updated with risk evidence.

- **Q2 (April – June)**
  - Mid‑year risk matrix refresh.
  - Security vulnerability risk escalation cycle.
  - Governance binder updated with mitigation actions.

- **Q3 (July – September)**
  - Risk heatmap review and visualization update.
  - Multilingual expansion risk assessment.
  - Investor Q&A session on risk governance.

- **Q4 (October – December)**
  - Annual consolidated risk review.
  - Board deck finalized with risk evidence.
  - Lessons learned integrated into CONTINUOUS-IMPROVEMENT.md.

Annual Risk Reviews

- Consolidated risk evidence presented in board deck.
- Strategic risk roadmap updated for next year.
- Continuous improvement cycle documented in EVOLUTION.md.

Ad‑Hoc Risk Activities

- **Critical Risks** → Escalated immediately with investor notification.
- **Security Vulnerabilities** → Disclosed per SECURITY-POLICY.md timelines.
- **Compliance Failures** → Logged in COMPLIANCE-CALENDAR.md and escalated.

Usage

- **Contributors** → Follow quarterly risk reviews in workflows.
- **Governance Leads** → Ensure risks logged and mitigations tracked.
- **Investors** → Review calendar for confidence in risk governance.

Review Cadence

- **Quarterly** → Risk calendar updated with reviews and escalations.
- **Annual** → Consolidated calendar presented in board deck.
