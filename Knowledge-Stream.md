Purpose
Provide a real‑time feed of lessons, competitor signals, adoption metrics, and market intelligence.  
Ensure PayMate AI adapts continuously and integrates live knowledge into agent workflows.

Evidence Flow

- **Knowledge-Stream.md** → Captures real‑time signals.
- **Knowledge-Agent.md** → Processes lessons.
- **Continuous-Improvement-Agent.md** → Applies upgrades.
- **Governance-Binder.md** → Consolidates evidence.
- **Investor-Packet.md** → Discloses confidence outputs.

Signal Types

- **Competitor Signals** → Market launches, rival features, pricing changes.
- **Adoption Metrics** → Usage spikes, retention drops, performance anomalies.
- **Investor Feedback** → Confidence trends, disclosure requests, Q&A.
- **Accessibility & Compliance** → Feedback from audits and user reports.
- **Systemic Lessons** → Captured from KNOWLEDGE-REGISTER.md.

Quarterly Review Checklist

- [ ] Competitor signals benchmarked.
- [ ] Adoption metrics integrated.
- [ ] Investor feedback aligned with disclosures.
- [ ] Accessibility feedback applied.
- [ ] Lessons mapped into Knowledge-Graph.md.

Outcome

Provides a **live company brain**, proving that PayMate AI integrates real‑time knowledge into governance, product, and investor confidence workflows.
