# Investor Calendar – PayMate AI

Purpose
Provide a structured calendar of investor updates, Q&A sessions, and packet deliveries.  
Ensures predictable cadence for investor relations and confidence in governance maturity.

Quarterly Investor Activities

- **Q1 (January – March)**
  - Investor packet update with Q1 metrics.
  - Investor Q&A session (INVESTOR-QA.md).
  - Accessibility and compliance evidence shared.

- **Q2 (April – June)**
  - Mid‑year investor packet with roadmap alignment.
  - Investor deck localized (EN/ES/FR).
  - CI/CD and multilingual progress update.

- **Q3 (July – September)**
  - Investor packet with fiscal and security updates.
  - Investor Q&A session focused on roadmap milestones.
  - Governance cadence evidence presented.

- **Q4 (October – December)**
  - Annual investor packet finalized with consolidated KPIs.
  - Investor deck presented alongside board deck.
  - Lessons learned and evolution cycle shared.

Annual Investor Review

- Consolidated investor packet delivered.
- Annual board deck presented with investor‑ready content.
- Strategic roadmap updated for next year.
- Continuous improvement cycle documented in CONTINUOUS-IMPROVEMENT.md.

Ad‑Hoc Investor Activities

- **Critical Risks** → Escalated immediately with investor notification.
- **Security Vulnerabilities** → Disclosed per SECURITY-POLICY.md timelines.
- **Investor Requests** → Addressed via INVESTOR-QA.md updates.

Usage

- **Contributors** → Prepare evidence for quarterly investor updates.
- **Governance Leads** → Ensure investor packet and deck updated.
- **Investors** → Review calendar for confidence in governance cadence.

Review Cadence

- **Quarterly** → Investor calendar updated with packet and Q&A outcomes.
- **Annual** → Consolidated calendar presented in board deck.
