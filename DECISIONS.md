# Decisions Log – PayMate AI

Purpose
Record key governance, compliance, product, and investor decisions with context, rationale, and accountability.  
Ensures transparency, traceability, and investor confidence.

Decision Categories

- **Governance Decisions** → Cadence adjustments, binder updates, role assignments.
- **Compliance Decisions** → Accessibility standards, multilingual expansion, regulatory alignment.
- **Product Decisions** → Feature prioritization, repo structure, workflow design.
- **Investor Decisions** → Roadmap adjustments, deck refinements, disclosure strategy.

Decision Lifecycle

1. **Proposal** → Decision identified via audit, risk, or feedback.
2. **Deliberation** → Governance team reviews rationale and impact.
3. **Approval** → Governance lead or board signs off.
4. **Implementation** → Contributor or officer executes decision.
5. **Review** → Decision validated in next governance cycle.

Evidence Sources

- **CHANGE-REGISTER.md** → Changes resulting from decisions.
- **AUDIT-REGISTER.md** → Audit findings prompting decisions.
- **RISK-REGISTER.md** → Risks mitigated by decisions.
- **LESSONS-LEARNED.md** → Insights feeding into decision rationale.

Review Cadence

- **Quarterly** → Decisions logged and reviewed in governance binder.
- **Annual** → Consolidated decision log presented in board deck.
