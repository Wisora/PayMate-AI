# Investor Sentiment – PayMate AI

Purpose
Track investor confidence trends across quarters.  
Provide transparent evidence for governance, contributors, and board oversight.

Confidence Levels

- **High** → Strong investor trust and adoption momentum.
- **Medium** → Stable confidence, minor concerns noted.
- **Low** → Confidence dip due to risks, delays, or compliance gaps.

Workflow

1. Investor sentiment captured during Q&A, disclosures, or market feedback.
2. Logged in Investor-Sentiment.md with confidence level and notes.
3. Linked to Investor-Register.md and Governance-Binder.md.
4. Consolidated in Quarterly-Confidence-Summary.md.
5. Reviewed quarterly in Governance Binder and disclosed in Investor-Packet.md.

Evidence Flow

- **Investor-Register.md** → Logs Q&A, disclosures, and sentiment triggers.
- **Quarterly-Confidence-Summary.md** → Consolidates sentiment trends.
- **Governance-Binder.md** → Consolidates investor confidence evidence.
- **Investor-Packet.md** → Publishes confidence disclosures for investors.

Outcome

Provides transparent investor confidence evidence, proving PayMate AI tracks sentiment systematically and discloses it responsibly.
