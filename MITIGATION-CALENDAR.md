# Mitigation Calendar – PayMate AI

Purpose
Provide a structured calendar of risk mitigation reviews and updates.  
Ensures predictable cadence for contributors, governance leads, and investors.

Quarterly Mitigation Activities

- **Q1 (January – March)**
  - Accessibility remediation plan review.
  - Certification renewal automation cycle initiated.
  - Investor packet updated with mitigation evidence.

- **Q2 (April – June)**
  - Mid‑year mitigation progress audit.
  - Logging automation rollout tracked.
  - Governance binder updated with mitigation cycle evidence.

- **Q3 (July – September)**
  - Risk mitigation heatmap review.
  - Contributor workflow mitigation updates.
  - Investor Q&A session on mitigation governance.

- **Q4 (October – December)**
  - Annual consolidated mitigation review.
  - Board deck finalized with mitigation evidence.
  - Lessons learned integrated into CONTINUOUS-IMPROVEMENT.md.

Annual Mitigation Reviews

- Consolidated mitigation evidence presented in board deck.
- Strategic mitigation roadmap updated for next year.
- Continuous improvement cycle documented in EVOLUTION.md.

Ad‑Hoc Mitigation Activities

- **Critical Risks** → Escalated immediately with mitigation plan.
- **Security Vulnerabilities** → Logged in SECURITY-CALENDAR.md.
- **Compliance Failures** → Cross‑referenced with COMPLIANCE-CALENDAR.md.

Usage

- **Contributors** → Follow quarterly mitigation reviews in workflows.
- **Governance Leads** → Ensure mitigations logged and escalated.
- **Investors** → Review calendar for confidence in mitigation governance.

Review Cadence

- **Quarterly** → Mitigation calendar updated with reviews and evidence.
- **Annual** → Consolidated calendar presented in board deck.
