# Metrics Dashboard – PayMate AI

Purpose
Provide a transparent snapshot of PayMate AI’s performance across growth, compliance, resilience, and contributor engagement.  
Ensure metrics are logged, tracked, and disclosed for investor confidence and board review.

KPI Categories

- **User Growth & Adoption** → Free tier, paid tiers, retention.
- **Contributor Engagement** → Onboarding, activity, retention.
- **Compliance & Security** → Regulatory coverage, audits, incidents.
- **Resilience & Improvement** → Crisis response, fallback activations, lessons logged.
- **Market Visibility** → Branding strength, campaign reach, investor confidence.

Update Protocol

- **Agents log metrics** into registers (QUALITY, LESSONS, COMPLIANCE, RISK).
- **COO Agent** consolidates contributor + adoption metrics.
- **CEO Agent** ensures investor packet reflects dashboard updates.
- **Governance Binder** integrates dashboard annually for board review.

Outcome

The Metrics Dashboard ensures PayMate AI is **transparent, measurable, and investor‑ready**, proving that growth, compliance, resilience, and community engagement are tracked and continuously improving.
