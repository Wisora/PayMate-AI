# Risk Register – PayMate AI

Purpose
Provide a detailed register of risks with severity, likelihood, and mitigation strategies.  
Ensures governance transparency and investor confidence.

Risk Categories

- **Governance Risks** → Cadence delays, binder integrity gaps, compliance misalignment.
- **Technical Risks** → CI/CD failures, dependency vulnerabilities, accessibility regressions.
- **Contributor Risks** → Workflow inconsistencies, onboarding gaps, commit discipline issues.
- **Investor Risks** → Roadmap delays, communication gaps, presentation inconsistencies.

Risk Scoring

- **Severity** → High, Medium, Low (impact on governance, compliance, or investor confidence).
- **Likelihood** → High, Medium, Low (probability of occurrence).
- **Mitigation** → Actions taken to reduce severity or likelihood.

Review Cadence

- **Quarterly** → Risks reviewed and updated in governance binder.
- **Annual** → Consolidated risk register presented in board deck.
- **Continuous** → CI pipeline monitors technical risks automatically.
