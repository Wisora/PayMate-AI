# Audit Calendar – PayMate AI

Purpose
Provide a structured calendar of internal and external audits.  
Ensures visibility into financial, compliance, security, and governance oversight cycles.

Quarterly Audits

- **Q1 (January – March)**
  - Internal compliance audit (accessibility, multilingual, GDPR).
  - Security dependency scans and patch cycle validation.
  - Governance binder review logged in AUDIT-REGISTER.md.

- **Q2 (April – June)**
  - External compliance audit (regulatory and accessibility).
  - CI/CD pipeline audit for reliability and rollback readiness.
  - Investor packet audit for accuracy and completeness.

- **Q3 (July – September)**
  - Internal security audit (penetration testing, vulnerability assessment).
  - Documentation audit for consistency and localization.
  - Governance cadence audit (risk escalation, board reporting).

- **Q4 (October – December)**
  - External financial audit.
  - Consolidated governance audit presented in board deck.
  - Annual compliance audit (GDPR, accessibility WCAG 2.2).

Annual Audits

- Consolidated audit evidence presented in board deck.
- External audit reports logged in AUDIT-REGISTER.md.
- Continuous improvement cycle documented in CONTINUOUS-IMPROVEMENT.md.

Usage

- **Contributors** → Prepare evidence for quarterly audits.
- **Governance Leads** → Ensure audit findings logged and escalated.
- **Investors** → Review calendar for confidence in oversight maturity.

Review Cadence

- **Quarterly** → Audit calendar updated with findings.
- **Annual** → Consolidated calendar presented in board deck.
