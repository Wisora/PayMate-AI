# Investor Register – PayMate AI

Purpose
Log all investor interactions, Q&A, disclosures, and sentiment.  
Provide transparent evidence for governance, contributors, and board oversight.

Structure

| Investor ID | Date | Interaction Type | Question / Disclosure | Response / Action | Linked Governance ID | Linked Confidence Summary | Owner Role | Status |

|-------------|------|------------------|-----------------------|-------------------|----------------------|---------------------------|------------|--------|

| INV-001 | 2026-06-15 | Q&A | "How is adoption trending?" | Shared Adoption-Dashboard.md metrics | CHANGE-REGISTER.md → CR-017 | Quarterly-Confidence-Summary.md → QCS-001 | Investor Relations Lead | Completed |
| INV-002 | 2026-06-20 | Disclosure | SOC 2 audit completed | Published in Investor-Packet.md | Governance-Binder.md → GB-011 | Quarterly-Confidence-Summary.md → QCS-002 | Compliance Officer | Completed |
| INV-003 | 2026-06-21 | Sentiment | Investor confidence dip after scaling delays | Logged in Investor-Sentiment.md | Governance-Binder.md → GB-012 | Quarterly-Confidence-Summary.md → QCS-003 | Governance Lead | In Progress |

Categories

- **Q&A** → Anticipated or actual investor questions and answers.
- **Disclosures** → Compliance, scaling, roadmap, or audit updates.
- **Sentiment** → Investor confidence trends logged in Investor-Sentiment.md.
- **Evidence Links** → Governance Binder, Change-Register, Quarterly Confidence Summary.

Workflow

1. Investor interaction occurs (Q&A, disclosure, sentiment).
2. Logged in Investor-Register.md with type, description, and linked evidence.
3. Linked to Governance Binder and Change-Register.md.
4. Confidence trends consolidated in Quarterly-Confidence-Summary.md.
5. Status updated (Completed, In Progress, Resolved).
6. Reviewed quarterly in Governance Binder and disclosed in Investor-Packet.md.

Evidence Flow

- **Investor-Packet.md** → Publishes polished investor disclosures.
- **Investor-Sentiment.md** → Logs confidence trends.
- **Quarterly-Confidence-Summary.md** → Consolidates investor confidence.
- **Governance-Binder.md** → Consolidates all investor evidence.
- **Change-Register.md** → Tracks systemic investor-related changes.

Outcome

Provides transparent investor evidence, proving PayMate AI is responsive, compliant, and confidence‑driven.
