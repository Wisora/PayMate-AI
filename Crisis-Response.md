# Crisis Response – PayMate AI

Purpose
Define protocols for handling unexpected events and risks.  
Provide transparent evidence for governance, contributors, and investors.

Structure

| Crisis ID | Date | Event | Impact | Response Protocol | Recovery Outcome | Linked Governance ID | Linked Resilience ID | Owner Role | Status |

|-----------|------|-------|--------|------------------|-----------------|----------------------|----------------------|------------|--------|

| CR-001 | 2026-06-15 | Database outage | High | Auto‑failover activated | Recovery within 15 minutes | Governance-Binder.md → GB-010 | Resilience-Dashboard.md → RS-001 | DevOps Lead | Resolved |
| CR-002 | 2026-06-20 | Accessibility audit failure | Medium | Remediation fallback triggered | Compliance restored | Governance-Binder.md → GB-011 | Resilience-Dashboard.md → RS-002 | Compliance Officer | In Progress |
| CR-003 | 2026-06-21 | Scaling delay disclosure | Medium | Investor disclosure fallback | Confidence dip logged | Governance-Binder.md → GB-012 | Resilience-Dashboard.md → RS-003 | Governance Lead | Logged |

Categories

- **Technical Crises** → Outages, scaling delays, CI/CD failures.
- **Compliance Crises** → Accessibility failures, privacy gaps.
- **Security Crises** → Vulnerability exploits, token breaches.
- **Investor Crises** → Disclosure gaps, confidence dips.

Workflow

1. Crisis event detected (technical, compliance, security, investor).
2. Logged in Crisis-Response.md with impact and response protocol.
3. Linked to Governance Binder and Resilience-Dashboard.md.
4. Recovery outcome documented and status updated.
5. Consolidated quarterly in Governance Binder and disclosed in Investor-Packet.md.

Evidence Flow

- **Resilience-Dashboard.md** → Logs fallback activations.
- **Governance-Binder.md** → Consolidates crisis evidence.
- **Quarterly-Confidence-Summary.md** → Confidence impact logged.
- **Investor-Packet.md** → Publishes crisis disclosures.

Outcome

Provides transparent crisis evidence, proving PayMate AI can handle unexpected events systematically and maintain investor confidence.
