# Financial Calendar – PayMate AI

Purpose
Provide a structured calendar of budgeting, reporting, and investor financial reviews.  
Ensures visibility into fiscal governance cycles for contributors, governance leads, and investors.

Quarterly Financial Reviews

- **Q1 (January – March)**
  - Budget allocation review for product and governance initiatives.
  - Investor packet updated with financial KPIs.
  - Expense tracking logged in FINANCIAL-REGISTER.md.

- **Q2 (April – June)**
  - Mid‑year budget adjustments.
  - Revenue and subscription growth audit.
  - Investor update on fiscal performance.

- **Q3 (July – September)**
  - Forecasting for year‑end and next year’s roadmap.
  - Governance binder updated with financial evidence.
  - Investor Q&A session on fiscal strategy.

- **Q4 (October – December)**
  - Annual financial consolidation.
  - External financial audit completed.
  - Board deck finalized with fiscal results.

Annual Financial Reviews

- Consolidated financial evidence presented in board deck.
- Strategic budget roadmap updated for next year.
- Continuous improvement cycle documented in CONTINUOUS-IMPROVEMENT.md.

Usage

- **Contributors** → Align spending with quarterly budget reviews.
- **Governance Leads** → Ensure financial evidence logged in FINANCIAL-REGISTER.md.
- **Investors** → Review calendar for confidence in fiscal governance.

Review Cadence

- **Quarterly** → Financial calendar updated with budget and audit results.
- **Annual** → Consolidated calendar presented in board deck.
